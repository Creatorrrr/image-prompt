# Blind pixel evaluation protocol

## Scope

Compare two anonymous first-attempt images against the supplied face reference. Reviewers receive no prompts, pack data, arm labels, generation reports, or A/B mapping. The reference controls facial appearance only; pose, wardrobe, setting, and composition are intentionally free.

Review each candidate first at thumbnail scale and then at native scale. Do not infer a real person's identity, age, or personality from the reference. Judge only visible image evidence.

## Hard observations

Record `pass` or `fail` for each candidate:

1. The primary woman is unmistakably adult.
2. The same clearly adult disciple or a unique repeated marker of that disciple is identifiable.
3. A concrete protective action is visible between the woman and that target.
4. The woman's cool surface and one localized warm affect leak coexist visibly.
5. A practical consequence for that same disciple is already visible in the frame.
6. The reference is used as facial appearance rather than copied pose/composition.

## Weighted score (100)

- Facial-appearance resemblance to the reference, including realistic adult eye scale, face length, lower-face/jaw width, lips, nose, complexion, and ash-blonde framing: 15
- Northern Sea Ice Palace world legibility: 15
- Rejuvenated ancient grandmaster concept, balancing youthful adult beauty with timeless authority: 15
- Cool-but-caring character response in one frame: 30
  - cool/composed surface: 5
  - identifiable adult relationship target: 5
  - target-directed protective action: 8
  - localized affect leak: 6
  - visible same-target consequence: 6
- Composition, focal hierarchy, lighting, and photographic credibility: 15
- Anatomy, hands, material continuity, and artifact control: 10

For every score, cite concrete pixel evidence and name the largest visible failure. Choose a preferred candidate for pixels only, or declare a tie within two points. Do not judge prompt quality, audit compliance, delivery reliability, or repeatability. One stochastic pair cannot establish causal superiority. Requesting-user preference remains pending.
