# Illustration Viewer Experience

## Compose One Viewer Promise

Declare one viewing context, audience-literacy scope, primary viewer need, and intended experience. These are design hypotheses, not predictions.

Ground the promise in:

- one first-glance hook;
- one different second-look reveal;
- a visible actor;
- a directed action;
- a visible target;
- a consequence or state change;
- an optional reinspection reward that changes the first reading.

A face, gaze, pose, color, shape, genre label, costume, or market term may support this chain but cannot replace it.

## Composed Evidence

Provide literal prompt phrases for:

- `first_glance_hook_phrase`
- `second_look_reveal_phrase`
- `affect_actor_phrase`
- `affect_action_phrase`
- `affect_target_phrase`
- `affect_consequence_phrase`
- `reinspection_reward_phrase` when required
- `commercial_legibility_phrase` when the objective is comprehension, memory, or action

The same second-look reveal must also appear byte-for-byte in `second_look_plan.reveal_phrase`. Bind it to two distinct visible consequences: a primary carrier and a safe fallback on another protected locus. Declare only the format inspection scales at which this secondary discovery is genuinely required to survive; every listed scale becomes a pixel-review obligation.

Do not write `the viewer feels`, `evokes empathy`, `creates attachment`, `memorable image`, or similar response claims as evidence.

## Audience Literacy

Use `general`, `genre_literate`, `subculture_literate`, or `expert` with a narrow prior-knowledge statement. General viewers should recover the main action. Genre-literate viewers may recover a secondary convention only when its scene context is declared.

Never turn a Korean, Japanese, Chinese, or cross-market convention into a national personality claim or universal dictionary equivalence.

## Commercial Objectives

Keep stopping, comprehension, memory, action, sharing, and return distinct. A cover or key visual may delay a secondary discovery, but it must not hide the title-safe hierarchy, subject, product, or story promise needed for its primary objective.

Local prompt and pixel review cannot prove population-level emotion, purchase, attachment, virality, or long-term memory.
