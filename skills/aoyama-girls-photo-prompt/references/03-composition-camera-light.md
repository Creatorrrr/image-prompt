# Composition, Camera, and Light

## Frame outward from meaning

Start with the meaning core, then test additions in this order:

1. adjacent gesture or expression
2. clothing or object in use
3. nearby personal trace
4. room or place structure
5. wider social environment

Stop widening when the next addition explains less than it decorates. Do not begin with a fully dressed set and search for somewhere to place the person.

## Meaning-led detail frames

For parts mode:

- identify what the detail says about action, role, time, individuality, or relationship
- include the smallest adjacent cue needed to make that meaning readable
- decide whether the face is included based on meaning, not mystery
- prefer an object-body or clothing-action junction over an isolated body fragment
- use 1:1 when concentration and interpretive space help; use another ratio when context is necessary

## Distance and viewpoint

Translate the internal viewer role into physical placement:

| Psychological distance | Physical suggestion | Typical effect |
|---|---|---|
| breath-close | very near, partial frame | trust, intensity, subjective attention |
| conversational | near eye level, medium frame | mutual presence and balanced context |
| across-room | doorway or room edge, wider frame | observation, boundary, solitude |
| remote | small figure in environment | memory, social position, anonymity |

Use eye level for equality unless the theme supplies a reason to depart from it. Avoid dramatic angles that create a power relation absent from the brief.

## Aspect ratio by function

- 1:1: a concentrated sign, hand-object relation, clothing-action junction, or interpretive detail.
- 4:5: portrait presence with enough editorial context for gesture and nearby place.
- 2:3 vertical: body flow, seated posture, doorway, or the vertical structure of a room.
- 3:2 horizontal: person-place relation, two zones of action, distance, or before-and-after context.
- wider horizontal: memory, isolation, or a small subject inside a social environment.

Honor a requested ratio. Otherwise choose the ratio whose spatial function best expresses the theme and state the reason.

## Focal length by narrative function

- 28-35mm: the room, route, or social environment must remain active; manage edge distortion.
- 40-50mm: ordinary proximity, conversational distance, and natural perspective.
- 65-85mm: individual expression or meaningful detail with modest separation; avoid erasing necessary place.
- compact-camera rendering: private immediacy, everyday record, and slight optical imperfection.

Never choose a lens only because it is cinematic or flattering. State what information the lens keeps or excludes.

## Action phase

Write the micro-sequence before the final prompt:

    previous_action:
    current_transition:
    next_likely_action:
    residual_expression_or_tension:
    camera_awareness:

The visible body should still carry the previous action while beginning the next. Replace generic instructions such as natural pose or candid smile with the observable phase.

## Light

Derive light from time, place, and emotional transition.

Default controls when the brief does not specify otherwise:

- one motivated window, doorway, practical lamp, or open-sky source
- side or gentle rear-side direction when it preserves shape and place
- moderate or low contrast with recoverable skin and fabric detail
- ambient fill from the real environment
- no clipped highlights that erase texture

Do not fix the skill to backlight, pastel color, shallow depth of field, overexposure, or one focal length. Hard light, flat room light, mixed practical light, or slight motion blur are valid when the theme needs them.

## Texture and postprocess

Use process texture only when it supports the proposition:

- retain skin, fabric, paper, painted wood, dust, and use marks
- keep natural asymmetry and small room irregularities
- allow restrained grain, color drift, or a slightly imperfect horizon only when meaningful
- remove generic beauty retouching, plastic surfaces, and excessive background blur
- preserve the real character of mixed light rather than forcing a fashionable grade

## Composition check

Before compiling, verify:

- the meaning core is visually dominant
- the room or place is recognizable for a reason
- the viewer's position can be inferred
- the action phase is not a completed pose
- no decorative item competes with the primary anchor
- the crop neither amputates relevant action nor widens past the information budget
