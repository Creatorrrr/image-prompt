# Maintenance and Evaluation

Maintenance-only. Never read this file while resolving a live request or writing its basic prompt. Maintained keyword meanings belong in structured runtime data and their tests; they must not be copied into `SKILL.md` or used as pre-core agent knowledge.

## Before Editing Data

Prefer extending existing entries with aliases, keywords, facets, and embedding text before adding slots or near-duplicate presets. Keep weights moderate and use filters plus facets for specificity.

Every preset needs non-empty `required_slots`. Reusable role recipes should separate `identity_core` from at least two `scene_variants`. Quality rules belong in `photo_prompt_quality_layers.json`, not repeated inside prompt templates.

Hybrid augmentation route definitions, adoption budgets, adult-appeal defaults and eligibility, carrier mappings, and cross-combination risk groups also belong in `photo_prompt_quality_layers.json`. Keep route slots backed by real dictionary slots and adult candidates backed by the declared `inventory_preset_id` filters. Add an axis candidate through that data contract; do not hard-code prompt prose in the generator. Preserve `sensual_editorial` and `fetish_fashion` as independent axes rather than a mutually exclusive mode enum; keep their configured defaults synchronized with the CLI constants.

V5 request-envelope, authorial-core, intent-lock, semantic-clarification, creative-augmentation, and downstream-intent-precedence contracts are topic-neutral control logic. Do not add topic-specific rewrites, keyword-to-safer-meaning substitutions, candidate-ID allowlists, creativity-to-example tables, fixed narrative routes, or pre-core keyword definitions. General invariants must enforce exact requester bytes, active-span coverage, provenance coverage, anchor coverage, locked/open dimension boundaries, non-revisable v2 meaning, and runtime hash binding for every subject domain. Every downstream semantic default must declare affected dimensions and be active only when all are explicitly open; suppression must remove its positive instruction, negative-prompt term, evidence duty, and render gate together. Locked evidence reuses the matching anchor exactly, while other non-open evidence comes only from the frozen baseline. Relevance comes from exact active spans with only true requester exclusions redacted, plus the frozen core and existing semantic scores; runtime-only labels remain available to retrieval/profile activation. Mandatory-intent evidence comes from positive semantic anchors so natural-language negatives are not inverted. Rule/lexical fallback is only for offline inspection. Meaning clarification remains deterministic; creative distance bands and seed sampling remain separate and open-dimension-only. Any new v5 public provenance field must be recomputable by `audit_composed_prompt.py` without exposing private scores. The envelope binding, intent lock, interpretation provenance, empty unresolved-ambiguity list, and downstream rule-state projection are part of that recomputable boundary; public-web URLs remain provenance only and do not enter retrieval text.

V6 replaces normal raw-text character-response classification with `photo-authorial-core/v3` typed `semantic_assertions`. The compiler may understand structural field names—actor, baseline, trigger, target, primary action, affect leak, visible response, consequence, continuity—but must not contain named-archetype branches, fixed gaze/pose geometry, or candidate-ID routes. Required values and evidence are authored pre-retrieval, source-span grounded, locked, and literal in the baseline. Exactly one required character-response assertion, one primary action, and one primary affect-leak channel are allowed. Every other required assertion must compile through the generic `photo-semantic-assertion-obligations/v1` contract and be recomputed by the composed auditor; adding a registry profile is not a substitute for this final-prompt binding. `request_lineage` hash-binds retries and keeps preserved dimensions disjoint from allowed changes. The normal v6 generation-and-pack path must be testable with the legacy raw moe router patched to raise; v5 retains that router only for compatibility.

BM25F is a generated projection of the existing authored dictionaries and visual-profile registry, not a second meaning store. Keep normalization, tokenizer recipe, field weights, document statistics, source hashes, and policy hashes in the generated indexes. NFKC/casefold normalization and whole-token boundaries are mandatory; Korean may emit conservative suffix- and plural-stripped stems, while Japanese/Han compound segmentation uses the authored longest-first lexicon and derives Han/Kana components from mixed-script aliases. Pair every intended inflection positive with embedded-substring negatives such as `외모에`, `규모에`, `용모에`, and `부모에게`. Exact matches may remain hard only where the authored contract already says so. BM25F, embedding, and RRF-fused hits are private ranking evidence and always advisory; creativity cannot harden them. Do not publish raw score, rank, matched terms, document frequency, or vectors.

For broad candidate coverage, add coherent subject/action/location/prop/surface clusters rather than isolated nouns. Maintain coverage across non-portrait families including ecology, technical systems, access and learning, sports mechanics, heritage, agriculture and food systems, lifecycle evidence, disasters, place change, natural processes, and reusable visual structure. Evidence-led typed packs are on-demand domains: automatic preset discovery requires an explicit routed domain intent, direct preset selection remains available, their tagged entries cannot leak into legacy presets, and semantic slot selection treats the preset filters as a hard record-coherence contract. ## Promotion Boundary

Local contract, scene-expression, contradiction, public generalization, and frozen holdout checks do not prove rendered-image quality. `render_scene_quality_holdout_v1.jsonl` freezes the cross-extension image cases but is not acceptance evidence until each case has a saved rendered result and pixel review. A `photo-domain-visual-review-plan/v1` file likewise defines pending cases and must never be passed off as acceptance evidence. Promotion of broad prompt-policy changes should also use the versioned `photo-visual-review/v1` artifact and fail closed on invalid enums, declared preset conflict, surviving body emphasis, missing fields, or missing provenance. Domain-specific `review_focus_results` are part of that boundary: every supplied focus needs evidence and a failed focus fails the case. Non-person operational reviews may mark body, role, or mixin fields `not_applicable`, but must include a case-level reason instead of using a meaningless pass. Run the combined boundary with `--acceptance-gate --visual-review <path>`; it requires the real semantic quality gate and a passing review artifact. Image/API generation is not part of ordinary validation unless the user explicitly authorizes it.
