The options are authored scene proposals. The research references support
theatre concepts and production context, not the exact proposed photographs.
They do not establish role-specific native qualification. A still cannot by
itself establish a performer's age, consent, intention, sound, historical
identity, cause or a transition over time. Preserve every stated actor, prop,
hand, support, direction, contact or gap and the boundary between a performer,
their reflection, a puppet or a shadow. A hidden endpoint is unobservable, not
a pass. Traditional forms and their roles must not be conflated or inferred
from one generic mask or costume.
