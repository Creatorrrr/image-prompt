# Military-fiction visual semantics

The advisory registry is
`assets/photo_military_fiction_semantics_20261024.json`. Its research graphs
and term routes support maintenance and source review; they do not run as
pre-core term expansion or supply typed native facts.

The 2026-10-24 extension preserves all 393 rows of the referenced military
glossary as advisory routes. It registers 103 new complete optional bilingual
visual sources, 52 excluded context diagnostics, 18 excluded held-topic
diagnostics and 203 optional role bindings. One complete trench-coat source is
reused. Two existing records receive narrowly scoped Korean translation or
aircraft-cockpit alias corrections, preserving their intrinsic conditions,
effects and provenance. Research material is in
`docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/military-fiction-20261024/`.

Military service, branch, specialty, rank, billet, legal status, experience,
competence, consent, romance, clinical conditions, sound and event history are
distinct from visible uniforms, bodies and equipment. Real state uniforms,
historical specimens, fictional designs and adult fashion remain separate
contexts. Genre keywords never create a weapon, battle, diagnosis, actual
affiliation, sexual act or exact insignia requirement.

Carrier pockets, armour inserts, chest rigs, PALS attachment grids, webbing,
slings, hydration tubes, handsets and helmet devices retain actual owners,
attachment surfaces, connection endpoints and mutually exclusive states.
Hands, connected joints, supporting bodies and surfaces must remain coherent.
Painted nose art and screen contents retain their own representation boundary.
External forms do not establish hidden armour, protective performance, sensor
accuracy, successful communication or the legal status of a depicted person.

The new records are authored alternatives, not official drill instructions or
validated specimen reconstructions. Sources support bounded terms and confusion
boundaries; their URLs and research graphs grant no native approval. Suggested
roles still require their complete current source and role contracts. Optional
material may be declined after comparison against the independently frozen
core; never use these research examples to write that initial core.

Registration, the sealed installed search release, actual prompt use, exact
reference transport, saved-pixel relations and requester acceptance retain
separate evidence. Current build, installation and independent three-arm image
test results belong in the research package's `INTEGRATION-RESULTS.md`, not in a
claim that registration itself improves image quality.
