relationship, gender pattern, consent, marriage and ending. Multiple people or
gifts alone do not establish a harem. A particular three-rival limited-choice
option never becomes the definition of every harem or reverse-harem scene.

is not a bare wrist; a stocking clip is not a thigh ring; a route panel or mirror
reflection is not another physical partner. All distinguishing components must
be observable together for a full saved-image pass.

conditions, effects, provenance and every other content field: a handkerchief

waist-support/strap/clip/same-stocking-welt boundary chain.
