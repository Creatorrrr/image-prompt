# Gaslamp Fantasy visual semantics

The 2026-10-23 authored extension retains 113 semantic cards, 217 advisory
keyword routes and 445 component/relation review gates. It adds 72 complete
bilingual optional visual sources, 35 positive-retrieval-excluded diagnostics
and 144 optional role bindings. Six existing complete sources are reused with
their original conditions, effects and wording. The phrenology-model card is
held pending artifact-level review. A mapping to an existing realization does
not silently add a qualifier absent from that source.

The authored files are `assets/photo_gaslamp_fantasy_semantics_20261023.json`,
`assets/photo_candidate_gaslamp_fantasy_20261023.json` and
`assets/photo_candidate_gaslamp_fantasy_bindings_20261023.json`. The candidate
and binding modules are registered in the corpus manifest. Each new source
also carries its reviewed owner/connection graph, source access scope,
confusion boundary, claim limit and gates in its research metadata.

Gaslamp/Gaslight Fantasy is a contextual genre description. It does not impose
London, fog, gas lamps, gears, dark clothing, gore or sexual treatment. Keep
gaslighting, market bustle, playing cards, visiting cards, dance programmes,
open-flame lamps and later mantle variants distinct. A print's represented
figure remains inside the print; a reflected figure retains its pane and
source; a conduit, wire, chain or pole needs its actual endpoints. One wrist
and another person's cuff are not interchangeable owners.

Material appearance does not establish fuel chemistry, jet composition,
laudanum contents, actual death, class, legal status, consent, coercion, lineage,
identity, sound, chronology or supernatural causation. The cited source access
scopes are preserved; authored geometry is not a copied historical standard.

These records grant no typed native facts, owner selections or whole-source
native approval. After independent core freeze, review complete sources and
their actual affected dimensions/properties through the managed workflow.
Role tags and advisory keyword routes do not authorize adoption. Keep complete
source constraints when using a compatible idea, and do not require an
unselected realization just because a genre term matched.

Source registration, installed search exposure, final prompt transport and
actual saved pixels are separate evidence. A hidden or partial endpoint is
`UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS`; `partial_is_fail` governs a complete case. The selected
`retrieval-runtime.json` remains the installed-release authority; editing the
authored source alone does not update its sealed corpus or vectors.
