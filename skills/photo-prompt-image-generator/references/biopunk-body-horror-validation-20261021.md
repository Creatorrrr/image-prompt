# Biopunk and body-horror validation, 2026-10-21

This extension contains 162 bilingual semantic cards, 259 keyword routes and 202
role bindings. There are 141 optional visual candidates: 135 still realizations
and six static proxies. The remaining 21 records are diagnostic/context,
sequence or audio material, outside positive image-material retrieval.
Twenty-six keyword routes intentionally retain scientific or critical context
without assigning a required visual card; appearance cannot establish those
mechanisms or interpretations.

## Scope of the source commit

The source and editable index are derived from the committed base plus only the
three biopunk/body-horror JSON assets. The corpus manifest adds only the two new
candidate/binding modules. Existing candidates, bindings, role specifications
and policies retain their exact content. The new index includes 18,509 positive
records and 91 diagnostics. Every referenced shard, global statistic, posting,
payload, vector extent and source identity is verified against that isolated
corpus. Existing document vectors and the 141 new document vectors are reused
only after exact text/recipe, model-space and execution-evidence checks.

No search kernel, ranking weights, source guard, native approval, automatic
negative policy or creative control is changed. No embedding, image or external
model review call is needed for this scoped rebuild.

The installed-runtime pointer is not included. The release used by the earlier
image experiment also contains separate pending source/code work. Committing
that pointer would include an unreviewed dependency on those changes. A clean
checkout needs a separately published and qualified installed generation to
expose these candidates through the default managed workflow. The current
shared working runtime is preserved.

## Earlier retrieval and image evidence

The earlier experiment ran against a broader local working corpus, not the
isolated corpus in this commit. Its source/index integrity, retrieval exposure
and image observations remain distinct evidence layers. They do not establish
the same retrieval rankings for this isolated commit or a later skill release.

A predesigned set of 84 development/boundary queries used raw whole-pool index
probes. Of 43 queries targeting a visual card, 28 exposed that target in top8.
Of 39 control queries, 16 also exposed new material. Two context queries had
their diagnostic targets correctly outside the positive pool. These are target
exposure results, not overall precision, all-path managed recall, held-out
evaluation or causal proof of an image-quality improvement. No data tuning was
performed on those results.

Three independent agents froze their cores and query intents before reading
their own candidate material. They used the supplied portrait only as an
appearance reference. Translation-exposed preparation agents and one
memory-exposed preparation agent were excluded from the final experiment.
There was one actual built-in image-generation call per final agent, with no
API/CLI/provider fallback and no additional generation.

## Pixel observations and limits

In C, bounded organic-looking tissue visibly enters a mechanical receiving
socket, and its boundary meets the metal rim at the same readable interface.
This supports that particular complete F08 realization. It does not prove
biological function, cell type, fabrication process or survival.

The existing C175 source's continuous tissue-ridge-to-tube-rib taper is hidden
or interrupted by a dark metal coupling. The normal left hand retained by the
authored core is absent or merged into the altered tissue. A separate lifted
gate cannot be confidently distinguished from the larger folds. Those partial
or unobservable obligations do not pass; technical qualification for C is
`fail`. The reference-like face/hair and the postal scene are visually supported,
without an identity, age, exact likeness score or user-acceptance claim.

## Remaining qualification

- Repeat managed slot/topic/baseline retrieval on a matching isolated installed
  release and a new untuned evaluation set. The earlier B/C `body_form` results
  were empty after source-witness projection, which does not prove corpus absence.
- Test more card families and shapes. Across the three runs only five distinct
  new cards were exposed, and final use concentrated on F08 and altered arms.
  This is not image validation of all 141 visual candidates.
- For permitted future geometry repairs, keep both endpoints, ordinary hands,
  continuous attachment transitions and distinct response structures large and
  visible in one image. Preserve locked meaning and owners; a changed owner is
  a new semantic request, not a successful repair of the old testcase.
- Keep the new sources `inspiration_only` until separate whole-source native
  qualification. An explicit aesthetic user judgment and a paired baseline
  image have not been obtained.
