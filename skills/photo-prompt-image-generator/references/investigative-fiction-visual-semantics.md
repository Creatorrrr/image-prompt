## What a still image can establish

Evidence markers identify locations or objects; they do not identify a trace
type or establish guilt. A gloved hand, a specimen container or a laboratory
bench is not proof of a completed analysis. A document-to-display comparison
can show matching visual marks or an act of comparison, but requires a separate
reading test to establish an exact account, amount, time or financial history.

A questioner, interviewee, photograph and route map retain distinct owners and
contacts. Their arrangement can show a question-and-response scene. Truth,
consent, legal status, coercion, the reliability of a memory and the success of
an investigation cannot be inferred from that arrangement. An isolated room,
locked door or clock is not proof of a locked-room mystery, an alibi or a
particular chronology.

Physical relations must be explicit: whose finger points to which mark, which
hand supports which object, where a ruler sits relative to the same trace the
camera records, and whether an actual person or a represented person owns a
feature. Props and genre lighting cannot substitute for those relations.
