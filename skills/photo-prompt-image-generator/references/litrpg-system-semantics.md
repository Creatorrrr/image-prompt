# LitRPG system visual semantics

The 2026-10-22 extension adds 60 complete optional bilingual visual realizations,
18 context/sequence diagnostics, 128 optional slot bindings and 297 advisory
routes (255 reference terms plus 42 examples/design questions).

`assets/photo_litrpg_system_semantics_20261022.json` retains the 78 research
cards, source access boundaries and adoption decisions. The candidate and binding
modules are `photo_candidate_litrpg_system_20261022.json` and
`photo_candidate_litrpg_system_bindings_20261022.json`.

Distinguish the information owner, perceiver, carrier, placement, resource or
rule function, and actual directed target. A screen corner, world-space panel,
physical card, skin inscription and equipment meter are different optional
carriers. Spatial placement does not establish who can perceive the display.
Displayed statistics, contracts, probability and affinity values prove what is
shown, not truth, causal efficacy, identity, health, willingness or consent.
Cooldown motion, head/world tracking, level changes, respawn and time loops need
sequential evidence or explicitly bounded depicted records.

Preserve each source's whole English and Korean meaning, exact owners/parts,
counts, materials and all connection/comparison endpoints. Existing D01-D08
sources retain their original adult conditions and complete source meaning.
New variants do not loosen those conditions. Research graphs and citations are
review metadata, without native facts, guard support, permission or whole-source
qualification. The broad LitRPG label imposes no blue panel, added person,
sexual tone, violence or game-specific palette. All suggestions remain optional.

`partial_is_fail` and `UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS` retain their ordinary meanings.
Source registration, index publication, installed exposure, actual prompt use,
saved pixels and requester acceptance require separate evidence. See the research
package at `docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/litrpg-20261022/` for full terms,
source access limitations, comparisons and evaluation cases.
