# Religious-habit and nun-inspired visual semantics — 2026-11-06

The semantic registry at `assets/photo_nun_habit_semantics_20261106.json` records
27 bilingual concept boundaries, 40 separately authored or reuse-review visual
relations, 12 diagnostic boundaries, and 20 advisory bilingual route pairs for religious habits, historical head coverings, devotional objects,
postures, fictional and adult costume, and visual state. Its N-coded rows point
to source candidates where available; N01 records an existing wimple composite
for reuse review rather than introducing a duplicate. The companion candidate,
optional-binding and retrieval-view modules are separate authored assets.

The conceptual and diagnostic rows and their keyword routes are advisory
research context. They neither authorize selecting a candidate nor impose
visual obligations. Preserve a selected source's complete selected-realization
meaning, intrinsic conditions, effects, and applicable role requirements.
Search retrieval, source eligibility, role qualification, an image's visible
pixels, and causal image-quality benefit are separate judgments. Hidden contact,
rear panels, counts, and material composition cannot be proven by a front view.

The scope is community- and period-specific: a veil or habit does not establish
belief, office, profession stage, age, or intent; a costume does not establish a
person's religious identity. A monastic cowl differs from a cowl neckline, and a
monastic scapular from an anatomical scapular region. A historical cornette or
Bridgettine crown is not a universal habit component. Fictional horror, armor,
violence and adult costume are distinct contexts with independently chosen
features. Historic artwork, community descriptions, and modern product
examples support only their stated evidence, not the full proposed scene.

See the local research plan and generated implementation evidence for source
scopes, duplicate-review limitations, corpus/index versions, search and image
test results. Semantic inclusion does not establish that every referenced
tradition, candidate, binding or installation has independently passed review.
