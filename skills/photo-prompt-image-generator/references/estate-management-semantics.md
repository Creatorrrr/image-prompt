An estate scene becomes legible through connected people, documents, work,
goods, supports and access paths. A ledger needs an actual writing or comparison
relation; an administrative desk alone does not prove administration. A boundary
map needs visible adjoining regions and identifiable endpoints; it does not
establish legal ownership or resolution. Clothing, headwear, tools and emblems
retain their own physical carriers rather than becoming proof of rank or rights.
Where a structure or mechanism is held for further research, its name does not
authorize an invented mechanical reconstruction.

Keep the following distinctions in prompts and evaluations:

- Material arrangement versus legal tenure, taxation rights, obligation or title.
- A document's visible format and contact versus its asserted contents or truth.
- An open route or gate versus lawful permission, lordship or consent.
- Food production and exchange versus dues, firstness, prior repair or chronology.
- Observed dress geometry versus historical identity, status, region or decade.
- A represented map, seal or depicted figure versus its real-world owner.
