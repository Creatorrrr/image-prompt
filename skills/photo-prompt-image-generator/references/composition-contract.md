# Composition Contract

## Candidate Pack v4

`--emit-candidate-pack` returns a JSON array. Generate and audit one pack at a time. The default pack carries `contract_version: photo-candidate-pack/v4`; `pack_id` is the first 16 hexadecimal characters of SHA-256 over canonical JSON with `pack_id` replaced by `null`.

v4 makes the agent the final author rather than a phrase assembler. Candidate labels, long candidate terms, photographic suggestion phrases, craft prompt fragments, and render-blueprint prose are not exposed as reusable final wording. Candidate surfaces contain unordered `concept_terms` and no sampler-selected flag, probability, weight, score, slot-level selected answer, or candidate-ID `covered_by` answer key. Candidate and route order is seed-shuffled and non-preferential: use individual technical, identity, or material terms selectively, but invent their relationship, causality, timing, and final prose. `authorial_composition.variation_lens` is a seed-varying abstract lens, not a mandatory phrase or scene.

v3 remains a replay projection for the immediately prior literal-blueprint contract, and v2 preserves its older public bookkeeping and profile aliases. Use `--candidate-pack-version v3` or `v2` only for a legacy consumer or historical reproduction. An explicit seed keeps those projections reproducible; an omitted seed now receives and records a fresh random effective seed.

v4 keeps only reproducibility, user-input, reference-control, and safety provenance. It withholds expanded argv, the private preset ID, sample-prompt hash, internal concept routes, duplicated character/adult routing, singleton preset candidates, and sampler-selected motifs. Its public `quality_profile` carries only request-critical subject kind; photographic integration categories and craft dimensions are unordered optional pools with no selected axis, strategy, refinement, score, or matched-facet answer key.

Sampler-built atomic scene groups are also private routing in v4. Their public projection is an `optional_inspiration_group` containing only non-ranked `allowed_candidate_ids`; the group name, selected entry, semantic entry-ID lists, scene-tag winner, and required-adoption rule are absent. Every group and every member may be rejected. v3/v2 retain the fail-closed atomic scene and selected-entry bookkeeping for replay.

If v4 would otherwise expose only one subject/action/location/prop candidate, it removes that singleton from `slots` and declares an `authorial_open_slots` entry instead. The composer supplies `authored_slots.<slot>.prompt_evidence` and `artistic_rationale`; a role-bound location also acknowledges its abstract `scene_family`. This preserves identity, no-people, safety, and role-family constraints without turning one sampled scene into the only permissible prose. When a private render blueprint exists, the broader `authored_scene` contract owns all four atoms instead.

The audit rejects a changed pack, a list containing zero or multiple packs, a missing composed field, a non-agent composer, an empty candidate selection, a changed negative prompt, failed concept gates, and a non-pass safety record.

Candidate caps are four presets, four candidates for core slots, two for support slots, and 64 slot candidates in total. Every sampled selection is reserved before alternatives so truncation cannot silently remove it. Ordinary slot alternatives come from the exact pool recorded after sampler applicability, no-people, compatibility, and hard-conflict filtering, with `applicability.source: sampler_eligible_pool`.

A resolved render blueprint remains private source material in v4. The public pack exposes only abstract scene functions, relationship stakes, genre anchors, visual-evidence types, and diegetic provenance. It never exposes the source subject/action/location/prop prose, blueprint ID, hash, or sibling inventory. The composer supplies a new `authored_scene` with a governing premise, artistic rationale, four newly written prompt-bound atoms, and at least two distinct interpretive choices. v3/v2 retain the prior four-literal-atom behavior solely for replay compatibility.

An explicit `--creativity` value from `0.75` through `1.0` may add `creative_exploration`. Its contrast rows never add, remove, or reorder candidates: each `candidate_id` is an already exposed `sampler_eligible_pool` option that clears conflicts and exceeds the declared feature-distance floor. v4 removes the prior `replaces_candidate_id` and relevance-rank answer key; use no more than `composition_guidance.use_at_most` mutually compatible options by artistic judgment. v3/v2 retain the replacement bookkeeping for replay. The field is absent below the activation floor.

The same explicit range adds `creative_direction`, an agent-level creation and selection contract with no topic examples or new candidates. It requires an ordinary-baseline critique, at least four proposals using distinct concept operators, exactly one selection, one rule change, at least two visible consequences, a staged reveal path, and a concrete authorial grammar. Its evidence fields must be literal in `prompt_en`; signatures from unselected proposals are forbidden. See `creative-direction-contract.md`. The field is absent below the activation floor, so ordinary composed prompts retain their existing contract.

`--hybrid-augmentation`, high creative direction, or an active adult-appeal axis adds `hybrid_augmentation`. In v4 it exposes three seed-rotated two-to-four-detail routes assembled from actual candidate IDs, but each detail carries only unordered concept terms. The composer may reject every route. When selecting one, every detail is `transformed` or `rejected`; one to three transformed IDs enter `chosen_candidate_ids` with an artistic interpretation, transformation description, transformation dimensions, marginal value, and newly authored literal evidence. Evidence made only by joining source terms fails audit. Active adult-appeal axes require an agent-authored axis interpretation, not adoption of a fixed inventory phrase. See `hybrid-augmentation-contract.md`. v3/v2 retain `accepted|modified|rejected` for replay.

All advisory v4 candidates are optional, including optional scene-group, visual-proposition, photographic-integration, and photographic-craft candidates. Hard identity, species-family, enforced role-scene, and safety constraints remain required when declared. For every chosen non-augmentation ID, `candidate_interpretations` contains exactly one row with an artistic interpretation, a transformation description, and literal newly authored prompt evidence. The evidence has at least four content words and introduces at least two content words beyond the source concept terms. An empty `chosen_candidate_ids` list is valid only when the authored request and hard contracts need no candidate.

`--viewer-experience` adds the separate topic-neutral `photo-viewer-experience/v1` composition contract. High creative-direction runs include it automatically. The contract changes neither presets nor slots; it requires one viewing context, audience-literacy scope, primary viewer need, scalar intended experience, visible first-glance hook, actor/action/target/consequence, attachment and reinspection conditions, and one commercial objective. Only declared visible evidence is literal in `prompt_en`; the intended response remains a hypothesis. See `viewer-experience-contract.md`. Ordinary requests without an explicit audience, affect, attachment, commercial, subculture-response, or creative goal retain the previous pack shape.

An explicit natural-language character-moe request adds `moe_response_contract/v10` and automatically enables viewer experience. The composed object must first preserve the routed adult aesthetic baseline (`adult_bishoujo`, `adult_bishonen`, or an explicitly requested beautiful-and-cute androgynous equivalent) and the routed relationship register, then provide literal adulthood, pretty/beautiful, cute/charming, and concrete face/hair/style evidence. Relationship semantics outrank compatible species reflexes: a mamang nekomimi request keeps `quiet_care_trace`/`nurturant_benevolence` primary and uses the ear reflex only as support. It must separately bind one warm affective leak that prevents guarded or negative expression from becoming the dominant face read, plus a plain/unlettered background control when text is not requested. A `denial_care_leak` must additionally bind a separate active mouth, chin, shoulder, or helping-hand denial cue, a visible lower care-action anchor, and one blurred partial outer eye plus temple/profile sliver from the same adult recipient at a named upper frame edge. The affection vector turns the primary head and nose three-quarter toward the opposite side while only the irises return to that visible landmark; softened lower lids and a starting mouth-corner lift are immediately suppressed. Looking only at the task, direct frontal eye contact, a centered selfie gaze, a passive side-eye, an imagined off-frame eye line, a second full face, or head and irises turning together is insufficient. Explicit mamang/maternal requests instead preserve `nurturant_benevolence` through one literal phrase combining relaxed brow, patient soft eyes, reassuring mouth, and protective attention. Ordinary requested care uses `directed_care_without_role_inference` and must not invent motherhood. The composed response must also preserve the routed primary mechanism and provide literal prompt evidence for actor, behavioral baseline, unfinished `event_phase`, trigger, target, visible response, immediate consequence, continuity, and a shared focal plane. Identity-controlled prompts additionally preserve eye aperture/shape/spacing, face length, lower-face/jaw width, and adult age with explicit anti-reshape clauses; a visible identity failure cannot be promoted as the representative candidate. The final English `prompt_en` must stay within the exposed 50–120-word budget. A generic person fails even with a good event; attractive or sexual styling fails without the pretty-and-cute gate and causal chain. See `moe-response-contract.md`.

## Required Composition Behavior

- Use every `mandatory_intents[].text` as literal or faithfully translated visible content.
- `mandatory_intents` contains required positive user meaning. Read `intent_contract` for the complete typed request: `user_requirement` and `additional_requirement` are required, `role_requirement` and `soft_guidance` are advisory, and `negative_requirement` is excluded.
- `audit_terms` and candidate labels are discovery aids, not proof of user-intent coverage.
- `coverage_assertions` may map an exact mandatory-intent key to one or more phrases. Every phrase must occur literally in `prompt_en`.
- Choose IDs only from `presets`, `slots`, proposition candidates, or the adult-appeal candidate inventory included in the pack.
- Choose only candidates with `applicability.status: eligible`.
- Do not choose both sides of a hard conflict.
- Preserve `negative_en` exactly, including `null`.
- Set `composer` to `agent`.
- When `viewer_experience.enabled` is true, add the composed `viewer_experience` object and obey its conditional evidence rules.
- When `moe_response.enabled` is true, add the composed `moe_response` object, preserve its primary/support mechanisms, and bind every required evidence phrase literally, including `affective_leak_phrase` and `background_control_phrase`.
- When `moe_response.reference_identity_control.enabled` is true, also bind `reference_identity_phrase`: name the uploaded portrait as the sole identity source, preserve at least three listed face anchors plus adult age, and forbid face substitution, structural beautification, and de-aging. Let expression, pose, outfit, light, and setting carry the tested change.
- When v4 `hybrid_augmentation.enabled` is true, add `augmentation_brief`, preserve its transformation budget, and bind every transformed detail with newly authored literal evidence. For v3/v2 replay, bind accepted or modified details under the legacy contract.
- When `authorial_open_slots` is present, add one `authored_slots` decision per listed slot with newly written literal evidence and an artistic rationale. A role-scene family named in the slot constraints must appear in `constraint_acknowledgments` as a contract acknowledgment, not prompt prose.
- For each ordinary v4 `chosen_candidate_ids` entry, add one `candidate_interpretations` row. Do not add rows for rejected candidates or for transformed augmentation details already documented in `augmentation_brief`.

## Masking and Generalization

Semantic dropout removes masked slot candidates, selected IDs, labels, terms, and preset values from the public pack. `open_slots` contains only the slot name, bucket, status, and reason. Fill the opening from the user request and remaining contract; never reconstruct the hidden exemplar.

If a composed ID names an open slot, the audit fails. Masked details are also removed from conflicts and mandatory-intent coverage metadata.

## Meaning and Coherence

- `intent_contract` and `coverage.intent_constraints`: typed source, polarity, subject category or exact curated subject route, domain, negative-presence constraint, and matching evidence. Excluded and advisory rows constrain or guide composition but are not positive mandatory prose.
- `scene_contract`: v4 candidate-backed groups are optional non-ranked inspiration pools with no sampler winner; they never require adoption. A v4 `authorial_scene` group instead requires newly authored subject, action, location, and prop evidence plus explicit interpretive judgment and contains no source prose. v3/v2 `atomic_scene` and `selected_render_blueprint` groups keep their fail-closed selected-entry and literal-label behavior for replay.
- `render_contract.selected_scene`: one selected scene function set, one diegetic visual provenance, relationship stakes, genre anchors, and visual evidence types. v4 exposes an authoring contract, not a source hash, blueprint ID, or atomic prose. Selection bookkeeping, source text, and available-blueprint inventories remain internal.
- `character_grammar`: an on-demand character route exposes one to three selected `visual_atom` nodes, one marked primary, plus visual evidence types and generic composition constraints. In v4, use these definitions and the abstract scene dimensions to invent the visible realization. Keep exactly one primary mechanism and at most two support cues. Runtime router anchors, compatibility-edge IDs, policy/guard records, identity, orientation, and adulthood policy are validated before the public projection and are never visual costume or morphology instructions. v3/v4 omit domain/topic/family IDs; v2 restores them only for compatibility.
- `--scene-function` is an optional control for direct taxonomy presets. It requires `--preset`, does not add a mandatory intent, and fails closed for an unknown or unavailable function. A no-people request first removes every blueprint that is not explicitly declared non-human; an empty or human-only remainder is an error rather than a silent human render.
- `evidence_budget`: count chosen slot names, not candidate count. A materialized scene prop may be the first physical clue; when the range is 1–2, choose no more than one additional listed clue slot.
- `concept_axes.required`: show each meaning axis through behavior, placement, expression, material, light, or framing.
- `role_scene_policy`: when `enforce` is true, select an allowed location ID; omission is a failure.
- `species_family`: select every required family slot from the allowed family. Missing IDs and mismatches both fail.
- `motif_budget.discouraged_now`: avoid repeated motifs unless the user explicitly requested one.
- `concept_gates`: machine-evaluable gates must be `pass`; the wrapper blocks failures before pack generation. A gate declared `manual` requires literal `manual_gate_evidence` with `review_stage: pixel_review_required`. This permits prompt preflight with a warning but never converts the pending native-pixel check into a pass.

## Moe Response and Event Timing

Keep a moe scene to one causal beat. Establish the character's ordinary rule, place a concrete trigger and target in-frame, freeze an unfinished transition, show one timed facial/hand/posture/reflex leak, and make one immediate consequence already visible. Bind the face or gaze, responding hand/posture, and target in a close or medium focal plane. For tsundere concealed affection, put the care target low in-frame and one blurred partial outer-eye-plus-temple/profile landmark from the same adult recipient at a named upper edge. Keep the primary head angled toward the opposite side and return only the irises to that landmark with one suppressed private-liking cue. Do not use the care target itself as the affection endpoint or show a competing second full face. Name a concrete state separation—open gap, partway contact, outside-destination object, or off-center trace—so the transition cannot read as a settled pose. For an ear reflex, show one trigger-side ear aimed toward the visible cause while the other stays at baseline. Keep intermediate props at ordinary scale. Avoid complete before/after sequences, settled handoffs, generic kindness, and attribute-only portraits.

Explicit nonsexual moe routes keep the subject clearly adult while suppressing the configured sensual-editorial and fetish-fashion defaults. Their generated negative prompt also suppresses generic sparkles, decorative blush circles, unrequested hearts, and comic reaction marks unless the source request asks for those graphic devices. Preserve `negative_en` byte-for-byte.

Plain adult-moe routes use `sexual_tone: sensual_optional` and retain the low-intensity `sensual_editorial=1`, `fetish_fashion=0` eligible-human default. Accept a candidate from the active sensual axis only as a subordinate gaze, pose, framing, lighting, silhouette, or mood decision. The composed prompt still fails if pretty-and-cute adult facial appeal or the character-specific event is missing.

Natural nekomimi routes are narrower than full beastkin. The enforced feline family exposes only a restrained species marker and organic ear-root connection; arms, hands, nails, face, and body proportions remain human, with no tail unless requested. A useful ear response is directional and asymmetric, not two symmetrically upright ears with no relation to the trigger. Inspect this distinction at native pixel size because prompt evidence alone cannot prove it survived rendering.

## Quality Layers

v4 publishes `quality_profile.profile_id: authorial` and only request-critical subject-kind facets; internal profile and facet winners remain private. v3 exposes character-mechanism routes as `character_scene_grammar`, while the v2 compatibility projection retains `character_moe_grammar`.

Use `photographic_integration.category_candidates` as a non-ranked pool and artistically select any fitting categories; use one visible `visual_proposition`, then at most one or two optional `photographic_craft.dimension_candidates`. None is a sampler recommendation. These are quality signals, so the audit reports omissions as warnings.

`artistic_final_touch` is disabled for most profiles. When enabled, use the sentence or equivalent terms; it does not have to be the exact final suffix. It is surface craft, not evidence of a distinct authorial point of view, and the creative-direction audit rejects using its fixed sentence as authorial grammar evidence. Never add the documentary imperfection formula to product, food, architecture, nature, or polished editorial prompts by default.

## Safety

Default safety is the small automatic-pass object documented in `SKILL.md`, with no approval wait. Run `--safety-evaluation` only when the user explicitly asks for a safety review. It changes only the report mode and lists evaluated recipe transforms; it does not grant extra authority and does not replace platform or image-tool policy.

Adult-appeal axes are composition inputs, not safety transforms. Eligible human packs use the configured `sensual_editorial=1`, `fetish_fashion=0` default; explicit controls may override or disable it. Permit `sensual_editorial` and `fetish_fashion` simultaneously when explicitly activated, but require an explicitly adult original subject, visible agency, and combined styling-pose-framing-camera review whenever active. A project audit failure must be fixed before generation; a project pass never overrides platform enforcement.
