# Dark fantasy and grimdark data integration

This is post-core reference material. Read it only after independently freezing
the scene; it does not supply an initial authorial baseline or add requester
requirements.

The 2026-10-22 addition registers 72 researched semantic cards and all 247
Korean/English keyword routes in
`assets/photo_dark_fantasy_grimdark_semantics_20261022.json`. The candidate
module `assets/photo_candidate_dark_fantasy_grimdark_20261022.json` contains
68 complete optional visual realizations and four context/diagnostic records.
The companion binding module adds 136 optional slot views. Diagnostic records
have no positive still-image bindings. A genre label does not activate every
associated object, palette, pose, moral interpretation or photographic method.

Each card preserves named components, directed relations, complete English and
Korean realization prose, confusion boundaries, source links, observability
limits and proposed pixel gates. The 46 sources support term definitions or
surrounding context. Exact fictional geometry is locally authored. The
provenance digest identifies the research specification, not cited webpage
bytes. The researched graph is review metadata and grants no typed native
facts, existing-owner permissions or native approval. New sources remain
unqualified; a retrieval hit or successful image does not qualify all cards.

Keep the original hollow, independently animated living armor separate from
wearer armor whose inner rim and tendrils meet the same wearer's skin. Do not
erase an original source's hollow interior or visible skin gap to reuse its
name. Keep a relic separate from its container; drainage through a gargoyle
separate from decorative grotesque sculpture; and a physical curse proxy
separate from proof of coercion, guilt, consent, kinship or historical events.
The four context records X05–X08 cannot establish such facts in one frame.

Under the current material workflow, review every returned source in full.
Use only a complete compatible realization as inspiration for open properties
of the independently frozen scene, or skip it. Preserve the selected source's
distinguishing owners and relations. Registration does not authorize a native
application. Record whether the selected source comes from this addition;
thematic resemblance alone is not evidence that the new data was used.

All required components and relation endpoints must be legible together in the
same original generated frame. Partial, occluded, tiny or unobservable evidence
does not pass a complete case. An observable symbol or trace supports only its
visible proxy, not a hidden event or moral conclusion. Separate scene-level
theme, selected-source realization, face-reference resemblance and requester
acceptance in reviews.

Research and implementation evidence live in
`docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/dark-fantasy-grimdark-20261022/` and
`generated/maintenance/dark-fantasy-grimdark-20261022/`. Development and boundary
queries are corpus probes, not independent held-out validation. The three
independent scene experiments under `generated/photo-runs/dark-fantasy-*`
provide only the individual source and pixel evidence recorded in their runs.
