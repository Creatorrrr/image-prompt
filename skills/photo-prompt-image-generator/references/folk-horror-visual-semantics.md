## Distinctions to preserve

- A holloway needs the road below the adjacent banks; a generic wooded path
  does not establish that geometry. A submerged causeway retains mainland,
  route, water coverage and island rather than just an isolated island.
- A Mari Lwyd is an authored skull-figure/pole/cloth/carrier realization.
  Skull, support and costume attachments stay separate from human anatomy.
- A sealed opaque protective bottle and an opened contents display are distinct
  complete choices. A source may not claim hidden pins or hair were visible.
- Architectural protective hexafoils, alleged marks on human skin, geometric
  exercises and decoration retain different surfaces, contexts and claim limits.
- Korean geumjul and Japanese shimenawa are separate cultural sources. Do not
  transfer pine/charcoal, paper shide or an exact regional configuration merely
  because both function as boundaries. Jangseung and sotdae retain face/post
  versus bird/pole structures.
- Gut performer, musician, instrument and offering table retain their owners.
  Cotton-knot gopuri, the symbolic mat/clothing/bowl structure and a long cotton
  path are separate selected ritual moments. A symbolic effigy is not a corpse.
- Kelpie, selkie, La Llorona and aswang/manananggal retain versions. Deer antlers
  or a deer skull do not follow automatically from the name windigo. Appearance
  does not establish a supernatural identity, consent, kinship, guilt or magic.
- Adult nudity, a genital amulet, fertility, sexual acts, initiation, coercion,
  endogamy and incest retain separate meanings. Visible depiction and historical
  testimony are not interchangeable. No category is silently deleted.
- Bog bodies, atypical burial, red liquid, injury, corpse treatment, sacrifice
  and cannibalism have distinct objects, life states, ownership and contexts.
  Still pixels cannot establish hidden causation, chemistry, sound or sequence.
