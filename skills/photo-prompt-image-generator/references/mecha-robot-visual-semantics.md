biological augmentation; links from joints, actuators and transmission; frame

jettison; power storage/conversion from propulsion and cooling; and a physical
scene from a display, cutaway, assembly diagram or sequence.

pilot hands and separate controls, boots and separate pedals, harness buckle
and seat anchors, service cable and both receptacles, leak origin and trail,
panel roots and tubing, lifting hook/straps/lifting eyes, human support on a
robot hand, and removed mechanical parts and their corresponding sockets.

belong to that separate person. A hidden endpoint or one local connector does
not prove a whole route. `partial_is_fail`; `UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS`.
