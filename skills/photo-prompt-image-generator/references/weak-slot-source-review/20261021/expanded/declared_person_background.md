## Primary research and epistemic limits

1. [US Census Bureau, Frequently Asked Questions About Language Use](https://www.census.gov/topics/population/language-use/about/faqs.html), read 2026-10-21. The FAQ separately records home language and English-speaking ability, and distinguishes historical childhood-language questions from present household-language questions. This supports preserving the actual language relation and time scope. It does not verify anyone's language history, infer fluency from childhood exposure, or prescribe a portrait. The proposed contextual portrait arrangement is authored.
2. [Stats NZ DataInfo+, Ethnic group](https://datainfoplus.stats.govt.nz/item/nz.govt.stats/aa22373d-9d10-4449-9b0d-572ec5c679fc), read 2026-10-21. Its description treats ethnic affiliation as self-perceived, permits multiple affiliations, and distinguishes it from ancestry, nationality and citizenship. These are definitions for this statistical system, not universal equivalences among every use of heritage. The proposed source keeps the requester's exact type of declaration rather than turning cultural affiliation into biological descent. No institution endorses the candidate or verifies an individual's claim.

The Stats NZ Māori population concept search result provided the same conceptual distinction, but its article open returned only an iframe. The fully read DataInfo+ definition is the cited primary support. No webpage content hash is claimed. Candidate provenance hashes the exact authored bytes of this review.

3. [US Census Bureau, Residence 1 Year Ago/Migration](https://www.census.gov/acs/www/about/why-we-ask-each-question/migration/), read 2026-10-21. It asks about the person's residence at an identified earlier time and separately asks whether they moved. This supports the distinction between a residence/time relation and inferred origin. The ten-year interval handling is an authored generalization, not a Census question or an individual fact. An initially guessed /residence/ URL was inaccessible; the actual /migration/ page was located, opened and read.

## Confusion boundaries and recommendation

No origin can be inferred from freckles, hair, dress, a nearby landmark, quoted book-cover words, or the photographer's biography. Explicit denial or missing biography is not a positive declaration. The book's named biography subject and the photographer remain distinct owners; relevant ordinary text, clothing and location advice may still be retrieved. Contrast probes do not demand blanket empty retrieval.

The actual 24-query baseline and zero-search production replay passed execution checks. Semantic review of all 106 unique top8 source records shows positive top1 fit 4/12 and top8 fit 6/12; contrast top1 fit 3/12 and useful ordinary-neighbor top8 fit 7/12. The strict direct background fit criterion does not count neutral portrait advice as a fit. Several negation/quoted/wrong-owner cases rank an affirmative main-subject biography source first. Therefore a justified generic correction is a conservative complete-source contract check: require affirmative same-person declaration scope; keep quotation, negation, owner, biographical field/time and visual-versus-context distinctions. Such a check must use actual selected positive source meaning and all conditions/effects, not CID lists, source tags, or declaration keywords alone. No code change is proposed here. The baseline is now reviewed in baseline-review.json; no post-addition retrieval or improvement has been measured. Existing fit counts do not establish native source eligibility or image quality. Data proposals remain uninstalled, unqualified and advisory.
