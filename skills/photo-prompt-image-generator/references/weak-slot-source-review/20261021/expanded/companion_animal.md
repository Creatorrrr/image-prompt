## Diagnosis

Mixed: a real generality gap plus owner/declaration recognition risks. Existing complete material already covers human–dog co-walking (cand_c914ed0216f54c6aa08e3410bfbe8401), the same dog's check-in to its established human travelling companion (cand_5c1f46ae57394732a59e0736bf642206), animal-on-human-lap support (cand_c03d4d952916403f81f3c568d49cc539), and companion-bird preening of a separate primary bird (cand_717b8c0986c94b30856169d5d416c329). Keep their native prerequisites and all pose, action, receiver and support effects. In particular, the bird-preening source is not a general bird-on-human source; the dog co-walk source requires a human primary and two already walking participants. The check-in cannot establish its own companionship prerequisite.

Short labels for named cat/dog variants, a nearby dove, parakeet pair, pet owner, playing with a pet and pet-on-lap remain narrower or incomplete material. A cat looking interaction at a seawall does not imply companionship. The familiar profile requires reciprocal attention, a shared signal and a joint task consequence; it is not generic accompaniment. A human in-frame companion record has a human condition and no explicit animal owner. No combination of these records licenses relaxing their owners to an unrelated species or dropping contact/action obligations.

Two missing reusable meanings justify two proposals: (1) a separately declared animal companion beside its identified primary, with both participants' existing activities and separate supports retained and without required physical contact; this can serve a resting human–animal scene or declared nonhuman primary without imposing human anatomy or dog walking; (2) readable bird-foot support on the already declared shoulder of its identified human counterpart, preserving an explicitly supplied perched scene. These are complete authored relation arrangements, not synonym variants. No separate cat, rabbit or horse copy is proposed.

## Primary research and epistemic limits

- [Cats Protection: Cats meeting other people](https://www.cats.org.uk/help-and-advice/caring-for-your-cat/cats-and-your-family/meeting-other-people) was opened on 2026-10-21. It describes variation in cat responses and allowing cats to approach people voluntarily. It supports avoiding forced contact or inferred affection. It does not establish any particular pictured animal's relationship, legal ownership, emotional state, cross-species friendship or the authored spacing arrangement.
- [RSPCA: How to train your pet bird](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/birds/training) was opened on 2026-10-21. Its bird-training discussion distinguishes stepping onto a hand or held perch, waiting and flying to a selected place. It explicitly discourages carrying birds on shoulders. Therefore the proposed shoulder record describes only an independently supplied still-image geometry. It is not a recommendation to stage that pose, a claim that shoulder carrying is safe, or sourced evidence that a real bird trusts a real person. The complete source restriction is retained rather than recasting the page as supporting shoulder handling.

The two candidate paragraphs, definition fields, application limitations and property scopes are authored maintenance proposals. The above organizations do not endorse them. Their provenance SHA256 identifies this review document; it is not a webpage hash, semantic proof, native owner admission, or adoption approval. No new image, model review, embedding or runtime was invoked by this review.

## Boundaries and possible generic correction

Negated animals and poster/album references must not create physical participants. A different walker's dog retains that walker as counterpart. Wildlife passing through the same scene and an animal alone do not establish accompaniment. A declared relationship is a prerequisite, not something inferred from visual proximity alone. Conversely, direct animal-to-primary relationship material need not imply human primary, affection, legal pet ownership, leash or contact.

If the actual baseline demonstrates false promotions, consider a bounded, source-witnessed advisory correction that retains the independent animal and primary endpoints, separates asserted physical participants from quoted/historical/negated mentions, and checks a selected source's actual accompanying/support/contact relation. No tag vote, CID list, absolute abstention for neighboring advisory material, or source-guard bypass is justified. Actual baseline quality and production replay remain separately recorded; these proposals are not automatically integrated or qualified.

## Actual expanded baseline

The completed baseline queried the full 18,419 positive CIDs / 18,421 RID documents for each of the 24 frozen probes. The 125 unique returned top8 sources were reviewed with their complete selected primary fields, conditions and effects in baseline-source-evidence.json. baseline-review.json records all 24 individual decisions. Positive direct/relevant companion material fits top1 in 2/12 and appears in top8 in 3/12. The declared dog check-in fits both languages; dog co-walk appears at rank2 for the English path walk but is missing from the Korean top8 despite existing in the pool. These observations establish an exposure problem alongside the two data gaps.

Both nonhuman horse-primary queries rank a human-only dog co-walk record first. Both quoted-poster queries rank a physical white-dog record first. The background-owner Korean contrast also returns the human-primary co-walk source as rank2, which cannot be applied to the standing foreground primary. These are evidence for a bounded source/owner/declaration-aware advisory correction. Preserve the genuine independent wildlife subject, pet-photo genre, typographic spacing and bystander-independence material where useful; the contrasts do not warrant universal abstention. All rankings and native-admission boundaries remain separate from manual source compatibility.
