## Research and epistemic limits

- [University of Illinois feline tarsus and foot atlas](https://vetmed.illinois.edu/imaging_anatomy/feline/hindlimb/tarsus_foot/ex02/tar-foot02-f%20.html) identifies an actual domestic cat hindlimb tarsus/foot imaging example. Its anatomy navigation distinguishes this from feline forelimb/carpus. The HTML was read; interactive radiograph pixels were not rendered. No exterior picture is claimed to reveal underlying bones, health or movement capacity.
- [Primary avian wing-morphing study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04477-8) measures elbow and wrist flexion/extension across bird species. It supports articulating wing segments and documents variation; it does not supply a universal joint angle or require that an observed bird fly. The shoulder attachment and selected visible route in the proposed photographic option are authored arrangements grounded in anatomy, not copied experimental observations. The NC State repository reference was bot-blocked and Michigan/Kentucky PDF fetches failed; they are not evidentiary support.
- [Iowa State leaf-petiole cuttings](https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/how-to/how-propagate-houseplants-leaf-petiole-cuttings) explains a removed leaf can retain its blade/petiole connection. The paired visible cut endpoints and placement beside an independently declared parent plant are authored staging. The article is not proof that a particular depicted cutting can root.
- [Minnesota Monstera propagation](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/propagating-monstera-deliciosa) gives the useful counterexample: a leaf/petiole alone does not imply new growth. The new botanical option therefore asserts only the declared cut topology, with no hidden bud, new root, viability or guaranteed propagation.

The proposals are discovery-only authored source records. No native approval, automatic qualification, changed query, ranking improvement, image quality or user acceptance is claimed. Conditions and application contracts must be fully reviewed during integration.

A possible future generic correction should require a positive same-owner and same-endpoint source witness with domain and representation compatibility. It must retain complete source effects and never infer ownership from tags, a lexical word or CID lists. A negated or quoted relation is not a positive biological witness. No code change is recommended from inventory inspection alone; the actual baseline must establish false promotion first.
