## Primary research and authored synthesis

[North Carolina Historic Preservation Office, Composition](https://www.dncr.nc.gov/historic-preservation-office/survey-and-national-register/2022-architectural-survey-manual-practical-advice-recording-historic-resources/open) distinguishes frontal architecture photographs from oblique views showing front and side elevations and recommends a level camera. Search returned the page text; direct open returned HTTP404. No webpage content hash is claimed.

[Nikon Professional Services, Split-Screen Display Zoom](https://nps.nikonimaging.com/technical_info/technical_solutions/d810_tips/split-screen/) was directly opened and read. It distinguishes pitch, roll and horizontal yaw when aligning architectural photographs, and separates lens movements from camera angling. This supports independent angular quantities, not mandatory symmetry, camera height or lens settings.

[Adobe, Dutch angle](https://www.adobe.com/au/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles/dutch-angle-shot.html) was directly read. Its geometric account supports camera roll and slanting scene references, distinct from an up/down tilt. Its coordinate-axis wording is not treated as a universal convention. The signed clockwise option fixes an operator-behind-camera viewpoint explicitly; sign and amount remain authored choices. Psychological associations and suggested degrees are not imported.

[Adobe, low-angle shot](https://www.adobe.com/au/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles/low-angle-shot.html) was directly read. It links upward aim with a below-subject viewpoint and notes that places can be subjects. The structural-underside relation is authored synthesis and only applies to an already present visible underside. No bridge, pier, base, sky, exact height, lens or emotion is created.

The options are 30–90-word authored positive photographic descriptions with faithful Korean translations and explicit application contracts. They are not copied test queries or synonym aliases. Their contextual requirements stay explicit and do not invent physical facts from a declaration, title, label, owner tag or score.
