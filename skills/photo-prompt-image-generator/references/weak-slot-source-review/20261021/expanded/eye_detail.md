The narrow gap is a reusable owned local texture or outer-border relation that preserves an already declared iris color. cand_7c1d71c236814ba5ba7a6d31e4a9e441 combines moss-green color, radial texture and a dark outer perimeter, with three appearance properties. [Statistics of Visual Features in the Human Iris, NISTIR 8386](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2021/NIST.IR.8386.pdf) documents visible iris landmarks and branching/overlapping stromal fibers. It supports local texture as a visible feature; it does not require one particular eye color or the proposed artistic spacing. [Appearance of the Human Eye: Optical Contributions to the Limbal Ring](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1097/OPX.0b013e3181b4f010) is an original human-eye experiment. Its abstract supports the visible dark limbal outline and an optical contribution to its appearance. It does not establish that the outline is necessarily a pigmented stripe or a cosmetic lens. [How the Eyes Work, National Eye Institute](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/healthy-vision/how-eyes-work) supports separating colored iris, central pupil opening and transparent cornea. These sourced anatomical observations are distinguished from authored arrangements such as a thin border or uneven visible streaks. Proposed arrangements are optional photographic choices, not medical assertions.

The two new candidates preserve the already declared human owner, iris color, side assignment, pupil geometry, gaze, expression and cosmetics. Their effects address only the named local texture or visible outer border. The border describes appearance, not its biological or optical cause. Neither candidate adds a person or eye, diagnoses health, establishes age, ancestry, personality or biometric identity, or authorizes contact lenses. Quoted titles and captions, a printed diagram’s eye, a gaze direction and a tear remain distinct owners or neighboring roles. Negated color changes do not become positive mutations; existing brown-eye preservation may still be useful ordinary advisory material. A metaphorical caption cannot override physically brown irises.

No CID-based rule, slot-tag vote, hard profile, native approval or runtime change is proposed. These data proposals require main-agent qualification and full-pool evaluation before integration. Source facts and provenance hashes do not prove native eligibility, rendered eye detail, or user acceptance.

## Genuine baseline review

The main worker ran all 24 frozen probes over 18,419 CIDs / 18,421 RIDs with 24 BM25F and 24 full local-cosine evaluations and 24 local query embeddings. Production replay passed without additional searches. I inspected complete positive-field meanings, conditions and effects for the 106 unique returned CIDs; baseline-source-snapshots.json retains the untruncated source snapshots. baseline-review.json covers all 24 queries and partitions every returned CID as compatible, incompatible or neutral. These are authored advisory judgments, not native adoption decisions.

Positive probes have useful role support at top one in 4/12 cases and somewhere in top eight in 5/12. Both languages expose central-color-ring and pigment-fleck material. The bilateral-color family is already present in the full pool but missing from both top eights, showing an exposure failure. The blue-radial request exposes green radial material only in English and no color-compatible local structure in Korean. Gray outer-border material is missing in both lists. The two narrow proposals address the mandatory-color coupling, not the independently demonstrated bilateral exposure failure.

The Korean negation probe puts five unequal-color sources before retained-brown-eye material at rank six; its English counterpart correctly places brown preservation first. Chart-owner probes expose actor-bound eye relations, which cannot be transferred to the printed chart or the lecturer. Caption and tear queries can legitimately return useful ordinary caption/tear advisory material; the eye_detail slot label does not invalidate relevant neighboring material. Accordingly, these contrasts do not demand blanket abstention.

A bounded general correction is justified for actual excluded color differences and owner/property confusion. Consider source-witnessed ordering that respects explicit negation, same-owner/property relations, and physical assertion versus quoted/declarative context. Require real positive source witnesses, preserve whole source restrictions, retain ordinary related material, leave raw query/embedding and full-pool scores inspectable, and do not use CID lists, slot-tag votes or a rule dedicated to these probes. The exposure failures need separate full-pool investigation; adding color-name aliases alone is not established as a fix.
