# Original 308 species-marker supplement review

All four old308 cases and all returned full selected-RID primary meanings, application contracts, conditions and effects were reviewed. Frozen 24 probes, 12 targets, four previous additions and their notes remain unchanged.

The declared generic fox cannot inherit the existing red-fox-only conditions, narrow-muzzle extra fact or fennec ear proportions. A new optional pointed-ear/bushy-tail ownership family preserves the independently declared fox species and existing colors. [Animal Diversity Web Vulpes](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Vulpes/) supports variable-sized pointed ears and bushy tails; the note does not claim these precise shapes universally identify every animal called fox. Explicitly requested shape compatibility remains a prerequisite.

The crane landing subject source supplies an event and a flock rather than the complete requested bill/leg ownership. Crane-operator, origami and embroidered crane sources retain their artifact owners. [Cornell Sandhill Crane identification](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Sandhill_Crane/id/) supports a long bill and long legs in an actual crane example. Narrow-leg outline and visible root readability are authored photographic arrangements, not a universal taxonomic ratio or declaration that every crane is a Sandhill Crane. The optional family requires an independently declared crane whose already requested shape fits, retains its exact species, and adds neither crown color nor flight, migration or flock.

The complete horse hoof/mane family is also absent. Hippogriff eagle forequarters, Pholos human legs, Sleipnir eight legs, lion-head hat and human transformation cannot donate isolated equine fragments. [Animal Diversity Web Equus caballus](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Equus_caballus/) describes equine hooves and mane hairs along the dorsal neck; breed, mane length/orientation and coat variation remain unchanged. Visible hoof-to-limb and mane-to-neck ownership is authored staging. The new family imposes no gait, field or saddle.

These are four source facts/arrangements across three species families, not native qualification. Source owner and part predicates, full effects and all application prerequisites remain cumulative. Quotation, costume, figurative animal names and neighboring owners cannot declare the biological owner. No fragment credit is given to extra-bundle sources. Generic definition-control has no concrete independent owner and needs no invented target.

Candidate IDs and target lists are fixed independently of draft rank and before any enriched retrieval on these additions. No retrieval/model/embedding calls were executed by this review. The old source inventory and actual modular preview were inspected; primary websites were researched only for factual source support.
