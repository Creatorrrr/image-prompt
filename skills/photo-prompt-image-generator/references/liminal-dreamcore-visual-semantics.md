Use a complete realization only when compatible with the independently frozen
scene and its open properties. A genre label does not require every associated
object or technique. Keep liminal use-state mismatch separate from damage;
warm dreamcore separate from threat; actual scene geometry separate from a
monitor, photograph or caption depicting it; and anatomical facelessness
separate from distance, blur or cloth occlusion. Keep water, stair, opening,
reflection and receiving wall attached to their actual respective owners.

All selected distinguishing components and relations must be legible together
in the generated frame. Partial, cropped, tiny or occluded evidence does not
pass the complete case. A still image cannot verify audible sound, elapsed
transformation, infinity, subjective emotion, identity, consent, or a place's
actual operating state.
