# Contextual expression after the independent draft

Use only when the pack declares `photo-contextual-appeal/v1`. Initial authorship
remains independent of candidate data. The frozen baseline records that starting
point; agent-chosen clothing, wearing method, gesture or light may still be
replaced in the final prompt within the declared scope. Requester locks remain
binding, including garment type, color and condition when specified.

## Interpret a possibility in this scene

The two axes are independent, overlapping readings of a whole portrayal.
Sensual editorial can emerge through dress, wearing method, posture, action,
gaze, light or distance. Fetish fashion can emerge through adult fashion's
material, construction, symbolic or garment–person–action relationships. Neither
an ordinary uniform nor a glove, pose or fabric has an intrinsic axis flag.
Consider what its use and visible relationships actually communicate here.

Search returns possibilities, not classifications. `contextual_status:
unassessed` is deliberate. Inspect full details for the bounded
`review_candidate_ids` and for anything adopted. The composer view exposes all
axis inventories, including whole directions, construction details and scene
relations; they are not limited to the general creative sampler's choices.
Read `contextual_usage` as optional examples and contrasts, and check
`context_requirements` before adoption. Ordinary readings and limits do not
count as positive retrieval prototypes or proof of the requested aesthetic.

Use the existing `adult_appeal_brief` to record:

```json
{
  "contextual_review": [
    {
      "candidate_id": "<supplied ID>",
      "reading": "potential",
      "reason": "What this candidate means in the current scene.",
      "proposed_application": "The concrete relationship possible within open properties."
    }
  ],
  "contextual_comparison": "Compare the baseline with viable alternatives at the requested strengths and explain the selected direction."
}
```

Readings are `relevant`, `potential`, `irrelevant`, `conflicting` or `uncertain`.
Potential requires a concrete application, not just relabeling an object.
Uncertainty can remain unresolved and the candidate can be rejected. Only
relevant or concretely developed potential candidates may be adopted. Existing
`candidate_interpretations` bind adopted ideas to final prompt evidence; do not
duplicate that evidence or add a separate score sheet here.

## Make the alternatives consequential

Compare coherent realizations at the **same requested strengths**. A different
way of wearing, acting or lighting can be a meaningful alternative without a new
garment. Tiny hardware substitutions do not stand in for a whole-direction
comparison when larger choices remain open. Judge appeal, emotional effect,
scene coherence and readability of the request together. Neither “the baseline
already reaches 3/3” nor a deliberately weak or excessive foil settles this.
If every supplied alternative conflicts or adds nothing useful, say why and keep
the baseline. There is no adoption quota or required outfit family.

Levels 0–3 describe no added treatment, supporting, clearly readable and leading
intent. They never unlock specific items or prescribe exposure. Keep each
candidate's full affected-property scope; do not narrow it merely to pass a
lock. A torn white dress can admit a compatible seam or drape change without
permitting a replacement dress, a new color or restoration of its condition.

The audit checks consideration, ownership and evidence binding. It cannot prove
the contextual reading, perceived strength, artistic success or final pixels.
Historical packs without this contract retain their original review rules.
