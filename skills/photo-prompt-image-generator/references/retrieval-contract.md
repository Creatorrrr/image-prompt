# Global retrieval contract

Freeze and validate the requester envelope, v3 authorial core, creative controls and embodiment review before reading candidates. The default public CLI searches every positive native record compatible with its independently frozen subject/context/exclusions. Slot tags describe roles; they are neither storage parents nor an admission filter.

One global record contributes one BM25F document and one vector. Exact cosine and lexical ranks are fused under `photo-global-retrieval/v1`. The public `photo-candidate-pack/v7` stores each retrieved payload once in `candidate_pool`; role and optional presentation views use native references. It admits at most 64 unique candidates with role caps 4/2. Untagged records are discoverable. Inspect the complete authored record and selected binding before adoption; a rank or tag grants no permission.

A private `photo-runtime-receipt/v2` pins source, binding, catalog, shared index, query vectors and algorithm to one immutable native generation. The auditor independently reconstructs allowed IDs, full-corpus lexical/cosine ranks and allocation from that exact vector. Missing query execution fails `hybrid_required`. Diagnostic `keyword_only` records embedding as not_requested. Neither lane absence nor a zero-hit executed lane is reported as the other.

Exact requester-defined profile meanings and complete opt-in contracts remain distinct from approximate discovery. Candidate retrieval cannot invent people, change owner/topology/direction, supply protected effects or turn an optional match into a hard obligation. All optional candidates may be rejected while retaining the independently authored baseline.
