# Maid-uniform visual semantics — 2026-11-06

The maid-uniform extension comprises 57 optional bilingual visual candidates,
10 context-only diagnostics, 57 optional bindings, and a reviewed retrieval-view
module with 57 whole, 57 general-context, and 28 specialized-context views.
See `assets/photo_maid_uniform_semantics_20261106.json`,
`assets/photo_candidate_maid_uniform_20261106.json`,
`assets/photo_candidate_maid_uniform_bindings_20261106.json`, and
`assets/photo_candidate_retrieval_views_maid_uniform_20261106.json`.
Diagnostics are excluded from positive visual choices. Source-grounded views
are search aids, not native approval or permission to alter frozen properties.

The authored propositions separate workwear, contemporary cafe uniforms, and
fictional costume, with apron coverage and attachment, cap and headband, garment
parts, materials, pose and object relationships, and selected genre variants.
Historical citations support specific garments and contexts, not the entire
authored scene geometry. An outfit or object alone does not prove occupation,
age, identity, consent, emotional state, or event history. Required parts or
connections hidden from view are unobservable. The source conversation's entire
term inventory and all existing candidate duplicates have not been exhaustively
reviewed; additional terms and sensitive variants remain subject to the local
research plan's individual review and scope boundaries.

In author-aware development checks, ten target queries retrieved their intended
new candidates within the top ten and unrelated control queries did not. These
are not independent holdout results. Three reference-guided image tests used
H-F2 generation `da8b1fbb41d697e2a36c86f5f7e2246beb4a74399c1a26326fad52724c55b093`.
New sources appeared in retrieval, but no complete new candidate was adopted.
Two runs used a partial general idea for refinement; the laundry scene had
unobservable clothespin contact, and the gothic observatory scene's gaze did not
follow the intended note. Three uncontrolled images do not demonstrate causal
image-quality improvement. The image files, prompts, receipts, and recording
limitations are retained locally under `generated/photo-runs/maid-20261106-*`.
