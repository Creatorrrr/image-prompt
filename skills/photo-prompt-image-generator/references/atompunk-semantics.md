# Atompunk visual semantics and optional candidate sources

The 2026-10-23 extension registers 88 complete bilingual visual alternatives,
13 context-only diagnostics, 176 optional bindings and 191 source-scoped keyword
routes. Its source registry is `assets/photo_atompunk_semantics_20261023.json`;
the two candidate/binding modules own operating source records. Older Atompunk,
raypunk, costume and material sources remain unchanged.

The alternatives separate reactor-like infrastructure and RTG-like power units,
detector probes and personal badges, cloud-chamber tracks and underwater blue
light, four-antenna satellite form and bulb-tipped radial lighting, ring stations
and domes, physical controls and numeral electrodes, roof sections and signage,
paint-panel ownership, domestic automation, clothing silhouettes, depicted media,
civil-defense spaces and fictional creature scale.

Source text is one complete optional branch. Read its whole EN/KO meaning,
conditions, effects, owners, counts and endpoint relations before use. Grouped
research labels are not synonyms: a minidress is a length, a shift is a cut;
metallic-looking weave differs from glossy vinyl; a six-legged insect differs
from an eight-legged spider. A printed person is not a second physical actor.
An appliance's two panels are not a whole-image color grade.

Source graphs, aliases, citations and review gates are research metadata. They
grant no typed native facts, source qualification, owner permission or automatic
hard-profile activation. The modern H-F2 material path retains its actual
`inspiration_only`/`skip` decisions and independent frozen core. Do not put these
material IDs in native chosen lists or borrow an unselected branch's properties.

The context diagnostics do not enter positive retrieval. They preserve historical,
political, strategic, audio, temporal, identity, diagnostic and consent distinctions
without turning them into a still-image recipe. Atompunk never automatically adds
a US diner, a catastrophe, green glow, sexual styling or every researched element.
Work-specific and material appearances do not prove provenance or chemistry.

Evaluate source registration, retrieval exposure, final prompt use and saved
native pixels independently. Every selected distinguishing component and
connection must be visible together; `partial_is_fail` and
`UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS` remain in effect. User acceptance starts pending.

Research originals stay under
`docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/atompunk-20261023/`.
Implementation, release and three independent reference-guided image tests are
recorded in its `integration-evidence/` subdirectory. The research originals are
historical inputs and do not become precore authoring material.
