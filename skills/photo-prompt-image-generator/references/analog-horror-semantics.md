# Analog horror visual semantics

This extension retains 78 semantic cards and 200 keyword routes. It adds 71
complete optional bilingual visual sources, 13 diagnostic/context records and
126 optional role bindings, reuses A064 without mutation and holds six cards
for more precise device, pattern or code references.

[The semantic registry](../assets/photo_analog_horror_semantics_20261022.json)
retains observable components, owners, directed relations, confusion boundaries,
source-access limits and proposed pixel gates. [Candidate sources](../assets/photo_candidate_analog_horror_20261022.json)
and [bindings](../assets/photo_candidate_analog_horror_bindings_20261022.json)
preserve complete authored alternatives and their intrinsic effects. The
registry and research graph are review metadata, not native qualification.

Physical recording media, displayed raster, surrounding room, document carrier
and representation of events are separate owners. Scanlines, dropout,
mistracking and head-switching appearance have different shapes and locations.
A still image cannot establish actual device failure, recording provenance,
audio, temporal continuation, identity, consent, life/death or supernatural
causation. A broad genre label never activates all cards or bypasses a guard.
Diagnostic records stay outside positive retrieval. Whole-source adoption needs
the actual source/native contract and frozen request permissions; exposure can
remain inspiration_only or be skipped.

[Research and integration evidence](../../../docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/analog-horror-20261022/integration-evidence/INTEGRATION-RESULTS.md)
separates registration, installed retrieval, literal prompt use and saved pixels.
Three independently frozen, reference-guided native calls returned three images.
One complete testcase passed. Two failed exact contact or mouth-state gates,
although broader topic relations were visible. New-source exposure occurred in
two arms. No controlled before/after image improvement, whole-corpus native
qualification or requesting-user acceptance is established. Keep
partial_is_fail and UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS.

The Git publication carries A064's pre-existing source and two original role
bindings as exact prerequisites because its initial Git base did not contain
them. This imports no other pending cosmic-horror records or source updates.
