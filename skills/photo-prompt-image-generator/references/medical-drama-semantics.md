# Medical-drama visual semantics

The 2026-10-23 extension separates a medical setting or role from the particular
visible action, bodily support, garment construction and instrument connection
that can actually establish the scene in one photograph.

Its registry is `assets/photo_medical_drama_semantics_20261023.json`: 172 research
cards and 346 advisory keyword routes, retaining the source scopes and access
limits in `docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/medical-drama-20261023/`.
The candidate and binding modules register 99 complete bilingual optional
visual sources, 51 positive-retrieval-excluded diagnostics and 198 bindings.
Twenty-two existing complete sources are reused without changing their payloads.
The diagnostics retain 42 context distinctions, eight reference holds and one
near-overlapping staged-set proposal held for separate usefulness review.

Routes suggest optional visual realizations; they neither define a compound
term by one component nor automatically activate a hard requirement. Context
terms such as diagnosis, authority, consent, malpractice and treatment success
cannot become visual proof through a white coat, a monitor or an expression.

For a chosen realization, preserve its entire English and Korean proposition,
all owners, garment layers, actual body parts, supports, directed contacts,
connection endpoints and explicit model, illustration or staged mode. A cuff
and a monitor without the complete connecting hose do not satisfy a connected
blood-pressure scene. A hand beside a gait belt does not establish a grip on
its handle. A screen is distinct from an upright surgical drape. An anatomical
model or a depicted medical image does not become a property of the actual
reference person.

The registry's owner graphs, cited definitions and observable gates are review
metadata. They confer no native qualification or permission to modify a frozen
requester property. Review the whole source and its conservative effects against
the current core, open scope and reference appearance before optional use.

Registration, installed full-pool retrieval, actual prompt use, reference
transport and saved-image results have separate evidence. Required contacts and
endpoints must be simultaneously visible at useful scale. Apply
`UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS` and `partial_is_fail`; a blocked generation remains
`BLOCKED_UNSCORED`. Neither a still photograph nor a source citation establishes
clinical correctness, internal placement, physiological readings, pain,
consciousness, credentials, identity, consent, chronology or user acceptance.
