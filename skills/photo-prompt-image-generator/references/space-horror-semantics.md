# Space-horror visual semantics

The 2026-10-22 extension registers 64 visual research cards, 20 contextual
distinctions and 200 keyword routes. It adds 53 complete optional bilingual
visual sources and 146 optional bindings. Five existing sources are referenced
without changing their meaning or conditions. Six deferred cards and the 20
context records are diagnostics, outside positive material retrieval.

`assets/photo_space_horror_semantics_20261022.json` retains owners, directed
relations, confusion boundaries, source access limits and adoption decisions.
The candidate and binding modules are `photo_candidate_space_horror_20261022.json`
and `photo_candidate_space_horror_bindings_20261022.json`.

Preserve every stated owner, count, component, material, layer and relationship
of a source together. Distinguish two-hatch transition chambers from ordinary
corridors; pressure, oxygen and carbon-dioxide indications from each other;
connected safety tethers from free ends; structural biological integration from
surface decoration; and sample containment direction from a generic laboratory.
An uncrewed spacecraft does not inherit supernatural properties of a maritime
ghost ship. A host, parasite, symbiont and parasitoid are separate concepts.

A static photograph can show a display, physical interface, trace or arrangement.
It does not establish diagnosis, cause, consciousness, consent, kinship, personal
identity, hidden history, a temporal change or physical measurement. Deferred
sequence, physics and perception claims remain bounded by their evidence needs.
Broad genre names prescribe no creature, gore, erotic treatment, palette or
specific geometry. Creative choices remain optional and agent-owned.

Research graphs and citations are advisory metadata, without native facts,
guard support, permission or whole-source qualification. Source registration,
installed retrieval exposure, candidate-informed prompt decisions, audited
transport, actual saved pixels and requester acceptance require separate proof.
`partial_is_fail` and `UNOBSERVABLE_NOT_PASS` apply to required visible relations.

Full research and integration evidence are retained under
`docs/research-evidence/photo-prompt/space-horror-20261022/`. The original research
files are historical evidence; later implementation and image results live in
