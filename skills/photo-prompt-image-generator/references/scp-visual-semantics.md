# SCP visual semantics and optional candidate data

The 2026-10-22 extension registers the bounded SCP research in
`assets/photo_scp_semantics_20261022.json`. Its 103 cards retain 268 original
glossary routes, actual source/version references, owners, directed relations,
confusion boundaries, observability and claim limits. This is maintenance
material, never initial-request precore inspiration or an automatic glossary
definition for a requester.

Two existing records are reused: the complete redacted-page realization and the
advisory antimemetics distinction. The former receives two faithful Korean
discovery aliases after a documented development-set recall repair; its original
body, conditions, effects and provenance remain unchanged. The latter is unchanged. The new module
`assets/photo_candidate_scp_20261022.json` adds 69 complete optional visual
variants and 32 diagnostic/context/sequence records; the latter are excluded
from positive material retrieval. The companion binding module adds 145
optional presentation relations. These counts do not represent native
qualification, generated-pixel success or requester approval.

Every positive record owns its complete EN/KO source and intrinsic effect
footprint. Apply actual source conditions, requester locks, ownership and
complete selected obligations. Graph metadata, source URLs, semantic titles,
aliases and slot tags do not grant native facts, permissions or authority.
H-F2 material use remains `inspiration_only` or `skip`; do not put an unqualified
CID into native chosen arrays.

Preserve these boundaries:

- Containment class, disruption, risk, personnel class, clearance and staff role
  are independent. Safe does not mean harmless and Keter does not mean strength.
- Cognitohazards, information hazards, transmitted memes, antimemetic suppression
  and counter-memes remain different. A pattern, black bar or bottle does not
  prove hazard efficacy, forgetting or drug action.
- GOC color types name abilities, not skin, garment or lighting colors.
- The existing `reality_anchor` slot means an ordinary comparison reference;
  the fictional Scranton Reality Anchor is an apparatus, not that slot.
- A robot hinge is nonbiological object structure. A photograph of an ARI
  headset is not an apparatus-view photograph. A recursive screen is not a
  mirror reflection. Inspect the actual catalog semantics before binding roles.
- Keep tissue/garment/tool ownership, both attachment endpoints, inside/outside,
  the physical carrier and its depicted image separate. Hidden required
  endpoints are unobservable; partial realization fails a complete case.
- A single frame cannot establish memory, faith, consent, fictional causation,
  temporal rules, death, real identity, social history or global catastrophe.
- Preserve source/branch/canon. Number001 alone selects no proposal. Do not
  merge Bright and Shaw as one canonical alias or infer an unknown SCP-055 form.
- The Alleys is a web-community name. Seocheon Country Club's name alone does
  not establish a golf course. Korean branch authorship does not prescribe
  discovery location, ethnicity, hanbok or shamanic scenery.
- 110-Montauk does not authorize inventing redacted acts. Keep sexual coercion,
  reproductive horror, institutional abuse and violence as distinct contextual
  meanings rather than turning them into erotic styling or an inferred past.

Specific source-scoped forms remain optional. A partial SCP-914 apparatus card
is not the whole article's fidelity contract; distance in a furniture store is
not infinity. The former SCP-173 photograph has separate rights from its text;
the new concrete/rebar variant uses an original design, not that photograph.
Attribution/ShareAlike and individual media rights remain in the research's
`LICENSE-NOTE.md` and source ledger.

Working source/index and the pinned installed H-F2 release are separate.
Source edits alone do not expose this dataset to managed image runs. Qualify
and install a matching sealed source/index generation, preserve existing
CURRENT and old runs, and record actual CID/RID exposure and final prompt use.
Research templates and a generated scene do not prove corpus-wide precision.

evidence for G08, R06 and I04. Registration-time pixel states are retained
separately. All three complete authored scenes failed a required relation;
I04's local specimen/vessel/support/gap relation passed in one image. Read
the exact pre-image gates and parent review rather than treating a local pass
as whole-scene or native qualification. Immutable candidate-embedded metadata
remains a registration-time snapshot.
