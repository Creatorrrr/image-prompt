# Illustration Format Contracts

## Single Frame

Require one decisive instant, a stable first-to-second-look route, and intentional visual rest or omission. Reject static lineups and lore exposition.

## Key Art

Require a clear project pillar, cast/world/stake hierarchy, and one repeatable visual signature. `ensemble_key_art` also requires distinct silhouettes and directed relations. `responsive_key_art` must preserve the core action and secondary clue in declared square, wide, and vertical safe zones.

## Light-Novel or Manga Cover

Require a story promise, character relation or conflict hook, quiet title-safe area, and trim-safe core. Do not generate readable title text unless explicitly requested and separately handled; decorative trope stacks do not count as a promise.

## Collectible Card

Require a frame-safe primary silhouette, visible hands/action/target, and effect causality that does not hide the event. Express rarity through scene scale, material consequence, or changed state—not paid-rarity UI, gem frames, or monetization cues.

## Vertical Scroll Sequence

Require at least two ordered beats or panels, gutter or spacing as duration, a delayed reveal threshold, and cross-panel identity invariants. A single poster stretched to 9:16 is invalid.

## Adaptation Board

Require at least two representations with declared identity invariants and format-specific simplification.

- `character_design_board`: invariant anchors plus intentional state or view changes.
- `merch_adaptation_board`: full, SD/chibi, sticker, or small-scale variants preserve functional anchors rather than shrinking all detail.
- `campaign_art_board`: different asset roles apply one shared signature differently; identical asset reuse is invalid.

## Aspect Ratio Is Not a Contract

Every format needs non-ratio evidence. A ratio may be added after hierarchy, crop, sequence, scale, and text-space behavior are defined, never instead of them.
