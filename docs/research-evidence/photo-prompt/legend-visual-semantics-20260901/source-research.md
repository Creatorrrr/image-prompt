# Legend visual-semantics research

## Scope

This note turns the broad keyword inventory from the referenced “전설 요소 조사” conversation into observable photographic relations. It does not treat `legend`, `legendary`, atmosphere, antiquity, costume, a named culture, or a supernatural object as sufficient visual proof.

The reusable proposition is:

`present site or object -> observable evidence or transmission relation`

or:

`past/extraordinary action -> continuous causal contact -> surviving or forming trace`

## Source-derived boundaries

- The [Academy of Korean Studies entry on oral narrative](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0029111) separates legend through a specific time/place, a concrete evidence object, a truth-oriented transmission stance, and local-community circulation. It also describes teller gesture, expression, audience attitude, and performance context as part of transmission. This supports a site-evidence-teller-listener contract rather than a mood label.
- The [AKS entry on warrior rocks](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0048593) treats a particular rock and traces such as footprints, handprints, or equipment associations as the evidence enabling transmission. This supports a fixed-rock, embedded-trace, scale, and site-reading contract.
- A [Dúchas Schools’ Collection record, “The Giant’s Bed”](https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbes/4798672/4789077/4892116), ties a specific hill and stone to visible finger and body tracks. It supports site specificity and physical-trace relations, while remaining one archival variant rather than a universal giant design.
- A [Dúchas archival questionnaire on prehistoric monuments](https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbe/9002152/7234799/9125289) records night digging for treasure believed to remain unsecured and guarded. This supports cache-attempt-guardian-failure causality, not a universal guardian species or ritual procedure.
- The [AKS entry on petrification narratives](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0064664) describes living beings becoming stone after an event, and the stone/site continuing as the narrative anchor. The existing continuous-metamorphosis profile already owns the source/bridge/target pixel relation, so legend data adds stone-specific optional candidates instead of a duplicate profile.
- [UNESCO’s Lake Titicaca account](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/protecting-underwater-cultural-heritage-lake-titicaca) reports submerged lakeside dwellings, multiple sites, and archaeological investigation. [UNESCO’s 2001 Convention principles](https://www.unesco.org/en/underwater-heritage/principles-2001) prioritize in-situ preservation. These support joined foundations, settlement plan, submerged occupation, and survey-scale evidence while rejecting generic fantasy palaces.
- The [NOAA maritime-heritage factsheet](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/fact-sheet/maritime-heritage-fact-sheet/) distinguishes shipwrecks, flooded landscapes, structures, vessel identity, use, cargo, loss cause, and site artifacts. It supports former-vessel identity and service residue, but does not itself validate a supernatural claim.
- [Royal Museums Greenwich’s ghost-ship overview](https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/maritime-history/halloween-on-board-cutty-sark) describes ghost ships as doomed to continue sailing and relates the motif to maritime risk, doubt, fear, and superstition. The new profile therefore requires a physically identifiable former vessel plus a bounded spectral breach, not fog or abandonment alone.
- The [International Society for Contemporary Legend Research constitution](https://contemporarylegend.org/society/constitution/) defines contemporary legend broadly as legend in active circulation in a community. Contemporary legend, memorate, variant, localization, and legend cycle remain advisory candidate vocabulary because their status cannot be proven by a single atmospheric frame.

## Runtime design

Six narrow exact profiles own five simultaneous component groups and five pixel gates each:

1. `local_legend_site_transmission`
2. `legendary_rock_trace_evidence`
3. `legendary_landform_origin_causality`
4. `guarded_hidden_treasure_attempt_failure`
5. `submerged_settlement_in_situ_structure`
6. `ghost_ship_former_vessel_breach`

`stone_transformation_trace` reuses `continuous_metamorphosis_source_target_bridge`. `prophecy_lineage_return_cycle` is candidate-only. Broad terms including prophecy, fate, lineage, returning king, contemporary legend, urban legend, memorate, variant, localization, and legend cycle never hard-activate these profiles.

## Evaluation boundary

Dictionary and prompt audits prove structure and literal actuation only. A rendered arm passes only when every declared thumbnail/native gate is visible in one saved image. `partial` is failure. The requesting user’s aesthetic judgment remains pending after technical review.
