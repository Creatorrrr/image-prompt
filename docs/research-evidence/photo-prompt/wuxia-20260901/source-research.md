# Wuxia visual-semantics and candidate-pack research — 2026-09-01

## Outcome

This research converts the broad vocabulary from the referenced **무협 요소 조사** conversation into observable still-image semantics. Its central correction is that wuxia is not adequately represented by a flowing robe, a sword, bamboo, and mist. The stronger model is:

> **human martial action + a readable jianghu social relation + a bounded event space + an observable consequence**

The change therefore separates reusable objects and environments from six all-of event contracts. Generic genre words remain discovery hints; only narrow scene phrases can create hard visual obligations. The selected contracts are rooftop qinggong traversal, a bamboo aerial duel, an inn identity standoff, a formal biwu salute standoff, a xia protective intervention, and a biaoju guarded-cargo departure.

## Evidence boundary

- Film, literature, museum, sport-rule, and academic sources can support a semantic decomposition. They do not prove that a generated image contains the required pixels.
- A source's description of wuxia, jianghu, xia, xianxia, or biaoju is not a license to copy source prose, characters, costumes, compositions, or branded imagery.
- A still image cannot verify internal energy, cultivation level, secret technique, moral character, faction loyalty, historical authenticity, weapon capability, or whether readable-looking text is accurate.
- Visible event geometry may support a protective or escort relation. Appearance, face, costume, title, ethnicity, nationality, or presumed virtue may not.
- The International Wushu Federation source supports a stable contemporary palm-and-fist salute relation. It does not establish a universal historical wuxia ritual.
- The xianxia source supports a genre boundary around cultivation and immortality. It does not imply that every supernatural or misty scene is xianxia.

## Genre and social-world findings

Stephen Teo's account frames wuxia as martial chivalry and the xia as a figure who fights injustice or rights wrongs. Scholarship on the imagined **jianghu** describes an unofficial rivers-and-lakes social space containing its own communities, schools, sects, clans, and rules. Together these sources support an event-and-relation model: the genre needs more than costume styling, but abstract righteousness or unofficial law still cannot be read directly from a single frame.

King Hu criticism supplies the most reusable visual grammar: sword heroines, secret allegiances, rooftop chases, tavern confrontations, bamboo showdowns, gravity-defying movement, mapped interior zones, delayed conflict, and action that remains spatially traceable. The useful abstraction is not a named film look. It is a set of observable relations: origin–transit–landing, opposed trajectories, separated faction zones, crossing eyelines, intact public routine, and bounded pre-contact distance.

The following terms may guide discovery, world selection, or scene expansion, but they are too broad to activate a hard obligation by themselves:

- 강호, 무림, 흑도, 백도, 정파, 사파, 마교
- 문파, 방파, 세가, 사문, 장문, 장로, 제자, 사형·사제·사숙
- 은원, 원한, 복수, 동맹, 배신, 약속, 명성, 소문, 정보망
- 비급, 전승, 사승, 파계, 출문, 개파, 무림대회, 무림맹
- 황실, 관부, 포졸, 금의위형 추적 세력, 변경, 관문

These become image-testable only after conversion into a concrete relation such as separate faction zones, an owned lineage object exchange, a witness-bounded contest, a guarded departure, or a pursuit path. Sect color, insignia, costume, or face cannot establish membership or loyalty without an explicit user-provided fictional design contract.

### Tier 4 — setting or narrative semantics that are not directly visible

These must not be certified from a still image and should remain prose context, optional prompt intent, or user-authored lore:

- 내공, 외공, 기, 진기, 심법, 운기, 주화입마
- 절정·화경·현경 같은 경지, 무공 숙련도, 깨달음, 잠재력
- 정의감, 협의, 의리, 충성, 배신 의도, 복수심, 명예
- 정체, 소속, 사제 혈통, 비급의 진위, 독의 효능, 치료 결과
- 명성, 소문, 정보 우위, 정치적 정통성, 공식·비공식 법 질서

If such a concept matters, the prompt must externalize it through props, positions, actions, and immediate consequences, while the evaluation names only those visible components.

## Six visual contracts

### 1. Rooftop qinggong traversal

Required components:

1. An old tiled-roof route with at least two separate support zones.
2. One complete, unmistakably adult martial traveler.
3. A readable launch surface and compressed or recently released takeoff relation.
4. A traceable airborne path toward a distinct landing zone, with coherent garment lag.
5. Exclusion of generic rooftop posing, parkour photography, flying-superhero posture, wire harness cues, and unanchored immortal levitation.

The hard claim is **traversal**, not supernatural power. A body floating above a roof without start and destination evidence fails.

### 2. Bamboo-forest aerial duel

Required components:

1. Repeated vertical bamboo trunks at several depths creating an open action corridor.
2. Exactly two complete adult rivals with distinct silhouettes.
3. Opposed rising, descending, or lateral trajectories inside the same corridor.
4. One shared exchange point plus separate branch, ground, or landing anchors.
5. Exclusion of a bamboo portrait, one-person sword pose, merged bodies, ground-only sparring, and free-floating xianxia tableau.

The bamboo must organize the duel's depth and occlusion; it cannot be wallpaper behind unrelated figures.

### 3. Jianghu inn identity standoff

Required components:

1. A sparse public waystation with wooden tables, an entry threshold, and stair or upper-room access.
2. Adult travelers in at least two separated doorway, table, or stair zones.
3. Physically compatible crossing eyelines and paused hands connecting the zones.
4. Upright, visibly owned bowls, cups, flask, or service traces preserving ordinary routine.
5. Exclusion of an empty historical restaurant, friendly meal, Western saloon, generic fantasy tavern, and open melee.

The profile deliberately chooses the moment before combat. Intact routine and spatially separated attention make the hidden-identity tension externally testable without claiming anyone's actual allegiance.

### 4. Formal biwu reciprocal-salute standoff

Required components:

1. Exactly two complete adult martial competitors as the only primary actors.
2. Both face each other and simultaneously hold a palm-and-fist salute at chest height.
3. One courtyard, platform, or marked contest boundary contains both competitors.
4. A clean center gap remains; witnesses, if present, stay outside the contest axis.
5. Exclusion of solo taolu, active contact fighting, boxing or MMA, military drill, handshake, and ceremony photography.

The generic words `biwu` and `비무` are intentionally insufficient activation terms. The hard route requires a narrow phrase naming the formal reciprocal-salute scene.

### 5. Xia protective intervention

Required components:

1. Three distinct roles: adult intervener, aggressor, and threatened traveler or civilian.
2. The aggressor's body and action visibly target the threatened person.
3. The adult intervener enters the same physical threat line between them.
4. A blocked, redirected, or delayed threat creates a visible escape opening.
5. Exclusion of a solo hero portrait, after-the-fact rescue pose, commercial escort formation, unrelated trio, and virtue inferred from appearance.

This is the most important evidence boundary in the pack: the data can label a **protective intervention event**, not a morally good person.

### 6. Biaoju guarded-cargo departure

Required components:

1. One loading yard or bureau gate opens onto the departure road.
2. Sealed boxes or bundled cargo visibly load one cart, pack animal, or coherent transport unit.
3. Adult martial escorts occupy front, rear, and flank positions around the same owned load.
4. Wheels, hooves, handles, and body directions continue from yard through gate onto one route.
5. Exclusion of an army convoy, merchant-only caravan, adventuring party, empty courtyard, and generic wagon.

The contract does not require readable signage. Institutional identity is supported by the complete cargo–escort–origin–route relation and remains qualified as a fictional wuxia scene.

## Candidate-pack mapping

The new candidates are deliberately distributed across slots so the generator can construct complete events rather than retrieve a single overburdened style tag.

| Slot | Added or narrowed candidate families | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `genre` | narrowed `wuxia_portrait` | genre context without automatic xianxia or cosplay conflation |
| `subject` | jianghu wanderer, rival pair, sect disciple group, biaoju escort team, protective-intervention group | explicit adult count and event roles |
| `costume_style` | narrowed `flowing_wuxia_robe` | layered travel morphology and coherent motion response |
| `action` | rooftop traversal, bamboo duel, inn standoff, reciprocal biwu salute, protective interposition, convoy departure, sect drill | relation owner for every selected scene |
| `prop` | sheathed-jian travel bundle, blank invitation token, loaded sealed cargo, training rack and drum, inn meal set, wrapped manual and lineage token | visible ownership and institutional or social context without readable text |
| `location` | tiled-roof route, bamboo action corridor, frontier inn, biwu courtyard, sect gate, biaoju yard-to-road, mountain-pass waystation | bounded event geography |
| `world` | jianghu unofficial social world | broad discovery context only |
| `lighting` | moonlit roof edge, bamboo canopy dappling, inn threshold faction light | depth and zone separation rather than genre proof |
| `composition` | start–transit–destination, inn faction triangle, bilateral witness frame, three-role intervention | preserves event geometry |
| `motion` | takeoff–landing fabric lag, opposed vertical reversal | makes trajectories traceable |

Legacy `wuxia_portrait`, `wuxia_cosplay_model`, and `flowing_wuxia_robe` text no longer treats `xianxia` or `cosplay` as interchangeable with wuxia. The legacy misty xianxia realm now names cultivation-and-immortality context without using wuxia as a synonym.

## Confusion matrix

| Target | Common false substitute | Decision boundary |
|---|---|---|
| wuxia | xianxia cultivation tableau | human social conflict and martial relation versus cultivation, immortality, alchemy, or unanchored transcendence |
| wuxia | generic kung-fu portrait | jianghu event and role relation versus martial pose or technique demonstration only |
| wuxia | historical-costume portrait | action path, ownership, or social geometry versus costume styling only |
| qinggong | levitation | visible launch and destination support versus unanchored suspension |
| qinggong | modern parkour | fictional tiled-roof martial scene with coherent robe and scabbard response versus contemporary sport capture |
| bamboo duel | scenic bamboo portrait | two opposed trajectories and one shared exchange point versus decorative background |
| inn standoff | tavern meal or brawl | separated zones, crossing attention, paused hands, and intact routine versus friendliness or already-collapsed melee |
| biwu | solo routine or combat sport | reciprocal salute, exactly two actors, pre-contact gap, and bounded witnesses versus performance or modern ring framing |
| xia intervention | heroic appearance | directed threat, physical interposition, and immediate consequence versus moral inference |
| biaoju | army convoy or merchant caravan | commercial cargo ownership, distributed martial protection, bureau origin, and route continuity versus generic transport |

## Activation and retrieval policy

- Exact multilingual scene phrases can activate one hard profile.
- Broad words such as `wuxia`, `무협`, `jianghu`, `강호`, `xia`, `협객`, `biwu`, `비무`, `biaoju`, or `표국` do not activate a hard profile alone.
- BM25F or embedding retrieval may suggest candidates, but a hard profile discovered semantically still requires all component-evidence groups.
- Candidate IDs are neutral options. They carry no fixed recommendation score or hidden ranking.
- Text rendered inside the image is never required for the six profiles; blank or unreadable-safe tokens are preferred.
- A selected profile owns only its subtype relation. Existing anatomy, contact, identity-reference, and lineage gates remain independent.

## Qualification rule

Dictionary validation, exact-term routing, semantic retrieval, prompt construction, render-request audit, rendered-pixel review, and user judgment are separate evidence layers. The current change can qualify the data structure and routing only. A generated image passes one of the six profiles only if every profile gate passes at its specified thumbnail or native scale; partial evidence is failure. No render or user-aesthetic claim is made by this research package.
