# 2026 Korean summer dailywear: visual semantics and candidate-pack research

Reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Evidence boundary

This note translates the referenced trend conversation and current official Korean retail/editorial material into observable image contracts. Popularity evidence decides what to prioritize; it does not prove that a rendered garment has the requested topology. Runtime data therefore stores only visible garment length, overlap, cuff, contour, attachment, transmission, and material-boundary evidence.

The attached portrait is used only as an appearance reference for a clearly adult subject. The test does not infer identity, same-person status, biometrics, protected traits, health, attractiveness, personality, or nationality. A visual resemblance observation is supplementary and never part of the trend-profile pass criteria.

The conversation-added figures for harem/Aladdin trousers were treated as requester-provided research context. Because the underlying current official page was not independently resolved during this implementation, those percentages are not repeated as verified facts in runtime data or the evidence ledger. A museum collection record supports the generic gathered-ankle harem structure; current Korean popularity remains a scoped trend-prioritization claim rather than a topology fact.

## Prioritized domestic pattern

Official current sources converge on a domestic summer grammar of a compact or simple upper garment, a length- or volume-led lower garment, one visibly light layer, and one bounded feminine or playful detail:

- KakaoStyle reports rapid growth around capri searches and treats 7–8-bu capri and knee-length Bermuda trousers as current summer categories.
- Musinsa separates volume silhouettes and lingerie-core mixing as current summer styling axes; the useful visible split is curved lower-garment volume versus attached lace or sheer material evidence.
- 29CM summer edits repeatedly pair sleeveless tops with wide trousers, lace edges, wrap layers, or lightweight knit layers.
- KakaoStyle's footwear reporting supports jelly footwear as a strong seasonal candidate, while its July trend note supports a single lemon-colored garment or bag as a localized accent.

These sources support candidate priority and combinations. They do not authorize exact colorimetry, hidden bra cups, cooling, UV blocking, waterproofing, tactile softness, fiber content, or a guarantee that the shopper-facing label maps to one construction.

## Candidate-pack decomposition

The candidate layer is deliberately split by owner so a popular outfit formula cannot silently force every part:

- `wardrobe_style`: fitted tank + capri + low flats; fitted top + harem; fitted top + balloon trousers; simple top + wrap skirt; attached-lace camisole + wide denim; tank + lightweight summer layer + wide trousers; fitted top + Bermuda + slim belt.
- `garment_detail`: gathered waist/ankle cuffs; convex balloon seam/taper; wrap-skirt overlap/fastening; strengthened attached lace-trim edge.
- `silhouette_proportion`: casual capri landmark relation; harem volume/cuff relation; balloon convex/taper relation; compact-top/voluminous-bottom contrast; Bermuda knee landmark.
- `footwear`: strengthened jelly footwear; low-profile ballet-flat topology; thong-sandal toe-post topology.
- `surface_material`: strengthened openwork lace; molded translucent jelly surface; lightweight open-knit layer without performance claims.
- `wearable_accessory`: narrow lace scarf; localized stud detail; clear jelly bag with one attached charm or scarf.
- `color`: one lemon-yellow item against a neutral or denim base.

No candidate order is a preference. Seasonal search growth is kept in this research note and evidence ledger, not in candidate weights or final prompt prose.

## Fail-closed ambiguity policy

- Bare `balloon`, `jelly`, `wrap`, or `lace` words do not activate the garment profile unless the registry's full exact phrase is present; nearby component language is advisory.
- `harem pants` and `Aladdin pants` share a broad gathered-ankle volume contract, but an extreme dropped crotch is not universal and is not a mandatory gate.
- `cap tank` or built-in bra cup is usually hidden construction. No hard visual profile claims an internal cup without visible cutaway or construction evidence.
- `살안타가디건`, cooling, UV, waterproof, and comfortable are performance or use claims. Runtime candidates express only a visibly lightweight or open-knit layer.
- A generic `9부` label is not an exact capri hard term because retail length naming can approach the ankle. The pixel contract uses actual knee–hem–ankle landmarks.
- `boxer shorts` can denote underwear or boxer-style pull-on shorts. It was not promoted into a hard profile in this revision.
- Lemon is a soft color accent candidate; one uncalibrated render cannot prove an exact commercial color name.

## Limitations

- Retail search and sales changes are platform-, window-, and taxonomy-specific and do not establish a universal Korean wardrobe.
- Editorial merchandising pages show selected products and styling, not population prevalence.
- Museum objects establish observable construction examples, not current trend popularity or a universal cultural garment definition.
- A single generated sample per arm can qualify only that saved image under its recorded generator and reference path; it cannot prove universal model behavior.
- Identity similarity and subjective trend appeal require separate user judgment and are not inferred by this evaluation.
