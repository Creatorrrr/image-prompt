## Consumer findings and limitations

C20 ordinary membership, raw rank and final ordinary public semantic candidate content are identical in all 16 cases. The auditory target remains available in eight cases; specific ear-root sibling coverage and the absence of all species eligibility in other cases are recorded in review.json. Absence is not proof of auditory-versus-ear-root discrimination or general runtime protection. Four representative baseline compositions reject all optional candidates and pass full current audits.

C21 ordinary membership, public inventory orders and public semantic candidate content are identical in all 16 cases. Admission/source bindings differ between arms; semantic-content equality does not mean byte-identical packs. Fifteen raw orders are identical; generic_lantern_room_ko swaps the already-exposed lighthouse lamp-room target from rank 2 to 1 with its generic lantern-room sibling, without changing public inventory order or semantic candidate content. Studio and standalone-workshop fixtures keep the target absent. Museum fixtures still expose it at raw rank 2 in both arms, a pre-existing wrong-setting overlap from a Fresnel component. Generic lantern-room fixtures explicitly name a lighthouse and are not mislabeled as wrong identity.

All four-input and retrieval recomputation audits pass. Full baseline composition audits pass for the C21 studio and workbench representatives. Keeper and museum retain exact failures for missing active adult-appeal postcomposition review fields. Their controls and prompt prose were not changed. Proposal-focused audits are explicitly narrower and do not substitute for full proposed-index/source validation.
