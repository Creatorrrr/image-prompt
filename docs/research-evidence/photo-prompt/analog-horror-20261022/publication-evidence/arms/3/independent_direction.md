# Independent direction

The local random draw selected continuity/sign-off. The sector was coordinator assigned; all particular staging was independently authored from general knowledge before project-local retrieval. Candidate files, research scenes, previous prompts and other arms were not opened.

I compared a close booth photograph, where the real and televised mouth states can be compared, with a wide empty-studio view, where the person and live monitor would be too small. The close booth better supports reference appearance, the private discovery and the contradiction. A CRT held near face height and angled toward the viewer creates one clear comparison plane. Caption and lamp are supporting cues; the face disagreement carries the picture.

The emotional reading imagined in the draft is a continuity announcer realizing her sign-off has ceased to follow her. The later pixel review must distinguish a visible mouth-state disagreement and analog carrier from a story that only prose supplies. Saved creative controls remain unchanged.
