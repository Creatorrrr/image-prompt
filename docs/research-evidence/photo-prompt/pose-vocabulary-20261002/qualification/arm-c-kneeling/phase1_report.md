## Target pixel contracts

### half-kneeling

One knee contacts the ground while the opposite foot is planted with its knee raised; hips remain lifted rather than sitting on heels.

- Exactly one knee clearly meets the floor or pad
- Opposite sole is clearly planted forward and that knee is raised
- Pelvis is raised above the rear heel and torso is supported upright

Close wrong answers: two-knee kneeling, seiza sitting on heels, low squat with both feet on floor, lunge with rear knee hovering.

Required observable regions: both knees, forward foot sole/floor interface, pelvis/rear-heel relationship.

### OK sign

Thumb tip and index tip touch in a closed loop while the other three fingers extend.

- Right thumb tip and index tip visibly contact
- Their connected curve encloses a clear hole
- Middle, ring and little fingers separately extend from that same hand

Close wrong answers: C-shaped unclosed pinch, thumbs-up, pointing, finger heart, all five fingers open.

Required observable regions: right wrist/arm chain, right thumb-index contact, right three remaining fingers.

### V sign

Index and middle fingers extend and separate into a V; ring and little fingers fold into palm under thumb.

- Left index and middle fingers are visibly extended
- The two extended fingers separate into a V
- Ring and little fingers fold while the thumb crosses them on the same hand

Close wrong answers: two parallel joined fingers, three extended fingers, open palm, pointing, finger heart.

Required observable regions: left wrist/arm chain, left index/middle separation, left folded ring/little and thumb.
