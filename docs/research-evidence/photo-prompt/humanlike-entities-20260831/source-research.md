# Humanlike entity visual semantics research — 2026-08-31

This research supports five versioned visual-obligation profiles and their unordered candidate concepts. It does not make ontology, genetic origin, cognition, identity, or synchronization directly inferable from pixels. Where the underlying term is non-visual, the profile requires visible contextual evidence and records that limitation in its false-substitute gates.

## Biological human clone

- Source: NHGRI, [Cloning Fact Sheet](https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/fact-sheets/Cloning-Fact-Sheet)
- Abstracted dimensions: copied biological origin; phenotype may diverge through development and environment; clone status is not equivalent to compulsory pixel-identical appearance.
- Runtime consequence: `biological_human_clone_provenance` requires living biological bodies, one source-lineage relation, and documented genomic or tissue-culture provenance. Android production, ordinary twins, split-screen duplication, and visual identity alone fail.
- Limitation: a still photograph cannot independently verify genetic identity.

## Human cellular chimera

- Sources: NCBI MeSH, [Chimera](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68002678); ISSCR, [Guidelines glossary](https://www.isscr.org/guidelines/glossary-new)
- Abstracted dimensions: integrated cell populations from genetically distinct origins; cellular lineage is distinct from mythological hybrid morphology.
- Runtime consequence: `human_cellular_chimera_lineage` preserves coherent human anatomy and requires a dual-lineage tissue map, paired samples, or comparable scientific readout.
- Limitation: ordinary visible anatomy cannot prove chimerism without contextual scientific evidence.

## Anthrobot

- Source: Gumuskaya et al., [Motile Living Biobots Self-Construct from Adult Human Somatic Progenitor Seed Cells](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10811512/)
- Abstracted dimensions: microscopic self-assembled multicellular bodies; adult human tracheal-cell origin; outward motile cilia; non-humanoid morphology.
- Runtime consequence: `anthrobot_microscopic_ciliated_biobot` requires a microscope scale bridge, living cellular texture, visible surface cilia, and motile culture evidence. A miniature humanoid or metal nanobot fails.
- Limitation: generated imagery is an educational visualization, not experimental evidence.

## Human digital twin

- Sources: [Human Digital Twins: systematic review and concept disambiguation for Industry 5.0](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166361524001581); [A roadmap for human body digital twins](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44287-024-00025-w)
- Abstracted dimensions: a specific physical referent, continuing live state update, operational digital model, and returned prediction or intervention; static model, digital double, shadow, and twin are not one realism ladder.
- Runtime consequence: `human_digital_twin_bidirectional_sync` requires outbound sensing and a distinct returned update that changes the physical decision surface.
- Limitation: a still image can stage evidence of synchronization but cannot prove a real operational connection.

## Biohybrid robot

- Source: [Biohybrid robots: Recent progress, challenges, and perspectives](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36265472/)
- Abstracted dimensions: living biological material plus engineered components; functional interface; movement or sensing jointly produced by both material classes.
- Runtime consequence: `biohybrid_robot_living_synthetic_integration` requires living tissue, synthetic structure, an observable functional interface, and an independent robot body. Biomimetic shape, organic styling, cyborg implants, and sensor-equipped animals fail.
- Limitation: appearance alone cannot establish tissue viability or biological function.

## Cross-cutting observability boundary

- Source: Chung et al., [Anthropomorphism in human–robot interactions: a multidimensional conceptualization](https://academic.oup.com/ct/article/33/1/42/6762954)
- Abstracted dimensions: humanlike form and behavior cues are distinct from attributed mental capacities. Thinking, feeling, perceiving, desiring, choosing, memory, empathy, and agency must not become single-image pixel gates.
- Reuse note: only definitions, observable relations, non-observable boundaries, and false-substitute distinctions informed the profiles. No source prose, figure, stimulus image, person, dataset row, or protected design was copied into runtime data.
