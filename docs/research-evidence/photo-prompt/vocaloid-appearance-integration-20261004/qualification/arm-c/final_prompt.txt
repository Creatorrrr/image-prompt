A complex photographic study of a reference-informed model in a projection gallery during installation assembly: an adult woman, using the supplied reference for facial appearance, pauses while fastening the waist panel of an original synthetic song-performer costume. Her face preserves the reference's facial proportions, eye shape, nose and mouth; her eyes meet the camera with a quiet, attentive expression. Her single dusty-violet hairstyle has a compact cropped rear mass ending high above the exposed nape; two long temple locks flank the same face and reach her chest. Copper earpieces rest on her ears; one slender boom leaves her left earpiece and curves beside her cheek to end at the left mouth corner. A graphite sleeveless bodice rises into a standing collar, a narrow copper tie ending above its waist belt. Detached long sleeves expose upper-arm intervals and end at squared wrist cuffs. A short fan-pleated overskirt and translucent flexible side panel hang from the belt. Her left thumb and forefinger guide the panel tab into a buckle steadied by her right hand, with both elbows softly bent. The tab bends slightly between her fingertips; tiny folds converge in the overskirt fabric around the fastening. Her left bare foot rests nearer the camera on a flat fitting mat, its upper surface and five toes exposed and smoothly joined to the same ankle; the other foot supports a stable staggered stance. She and the unfinished display form a complex interrelated visual arrangement. A partially mounted translucent screen stands behind her on an aluminum frame. Cable coils, tape, paper patterns and cut material remain on a table beside her silhouette; floor-position tape traces the assembly area. A left three-quarter full-figure view includes both ankles and feet. Her head turns slightly toward the screen as her eyes return to the camera; a clear bare nape strip separates the high cropped rear hairline from the collar, while both long front locks remain visible. Broad neutral overhead light keeps the face and garment joins legible; a low amber work lamp gives copper seams and the screen an understated warm gleam. Skin texture, fabric creases and brushed metal surfaces retain the sense of a physical costume fitting.
