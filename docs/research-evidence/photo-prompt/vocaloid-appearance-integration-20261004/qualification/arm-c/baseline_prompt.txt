A complex photographic study of a reference-informed model in an installation gallery: an adult woman, using the supplied reference for facial appearance, pauses while fastening the waist panel of an original synthetic song-performer costume. Her face preserves the reference's facial proportions, eye shape, nose and mouth; her eyes meet the camera with a quiet, attentive expression. Dusty-violet twin tails descend from two angular clips above and behind the ears, their long sections falling behind her arms. Copper headset housings sit over both ears; a slim boom from the housing on her left ends beside the corner of her mouth. A graphite sleeveless fitted bodice rises into a standing collar, with a narrow copper tie laid down its center and ending above the belt. Detached long sleeves begin below the shoulders, exposing distinct upper-arm intervals; squared wrist cuffs finish before her hands. A belt supports a short fan-pleated overskirt and a translucent side panel curving toward a front buckle. At the front of the waistband, her left thumb and forefinger guide the panel tab into a buckle steadied by her right hand, with elbows softly bent and fingers visible beyond both cuffs. Graphite boots rise to mid-thigh, leaving a visible leg interval between each boot top and the skirt hem. Both feet rest on the gallery floor in a stable, slightly staggered stance. Her figure and the unfinished display form a complex interrelated visual arrangement. A partially mounted translucent screen stands behind her on an aluminum frame; a worktable to her side holds cable coils, tape, paper patterns and cut material, with floor-position tape extending toward the frame. Photograph her whole figure including both boot soles from a near-frontal three-quarter view, with the table beside her silhouette. Broad neutral overhead light keeps the face and garment joins legible; a low amber work lamp gives the copper seams and nearby screen an understated warm gleam. Natural skin texture, fabric creases and brushed metal surfaces retain the sense of a physical costume fitting.
