A layered photographic concept test featuring a reference-guided portrait subject, captured in one still photograph. An adult female costume model stands beside the inclined console in a contemporary recording control room. The supplied image guides her facial appearance, retaining the visible face contour, eye spacing, nose shape and softly shaped lips. Her black costume wig divides into two long high side tails; a rigid open rectangular hair frame brackets the root of each tail. A close-fitting headset has two earpieces and a short boom curving from her left earpiece toward the corner of her mouth. Her original charcoal, wine-red and pale-nickel synth-singer outfit connects a standing collar to a sleeveless tapered bodice with a narrow front tie. Separate ribbed forearm sleeves end below the upper arms, leaving a clear separation from the bodice armholes. A waist-mounted belt supports a short pleated skirt with a translucent stepped hem. Opaque thigh-high stockings stop below the skirt hem, leaving a visible interval between the two garment edges. These relationships form one continuous performance silhouette. Her left fingertips rest on a single fader; the console enters at the right edge of the frame beside her left hip. Both feet support her on the floor, weight mainly over the right leg, and her right arm hangs naturally beside the skirt. Her shoulders turn toward the camera after one final fader adjustment; her eyes meet the lens with a small, contained smile. Behind her, a booth window, dark acoustic fins and low glowing level meters make the recent listening session legible. Pale window light after rain crosses her face and reveals the costume's seams, while a low amber practical light edges the two hair tails. A vertical full-length portrait from chest height, with natural 50 mm perspective, gives her face first emphasis, then the headset and linked costume layers; the shallow console diagonal draws the eye back to her.
