A layered photographic concept test featuring a reference-guided portrait subject, captured in one still photograph. An adult female costume model stands beside the inclined console in a contemporary recording control room. The supplied image guides her facial appearance, retaining the face contour, eye spacing, nose shape and softly shaped lips. Her black costume wig divides into two long high side tails; a rigid open rectangular hair frame brackets the root of each tail. A pale-nickel headset sits on her ears; a slim microphone boom leaves her left earpiece, curves along her cheek and places its tip beside the lip corner of this same model. Her original charcoal, wine-red and pale-nickel synth-singer outfit connects a standing collar to a sleeveless tapered bodice with a narrow front tie. Separate ribbed forearm sleeves end below the upper arms, leaving a clear separation from the bodice armholes. A waist-mounted belt supports a short pleated skirt with a translucent stepped hem. Pale window light after rain crosses her face and reveals the costume's seams; a low amber practical light edges the two hair tails.
