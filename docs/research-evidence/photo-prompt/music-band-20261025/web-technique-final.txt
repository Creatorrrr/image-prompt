The cross-stick feature on the TD-07KV allows you to strike the rim of the snare pad and trigger a cross-stick sound. When playing a cross-stick, lay the stick from rim to rim on the snare pad and tap the rim. Laying one end of the stick on the head may not produce the cross-stick sound.

  1. On the TD-07, press the [Image: TD-07KV_setupbutton.jpg] (SETUP) button.
  2. Use the [◀] [▶] buttons to select “PAD,” press the [Image: TD-07KV_enterbutton.jpg] (ENTER) button.
The PAD screen appears.
  3. Hit the snare pad to select it.
  4. Use the [◀] [▶] buttons to select "XStickSens," and turn the main dial to select the desired value (OFF, 1–10). A higher value makes it easier to perform cross sticks with lighter taps.
  5. Press the [Image: TD-07KV_drumkitbutton.jpg] (DRUM KIT) button to return to the DRUM KIT Screen.
The new setting is saved in the Kit automatically.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
