The monolithic unittest discovery was terminated with signal 9 after 3,208 seconds,
without a unittest footer. The cause is unverified. Its original log and receipt
remain intact; post-exit available memory and disk do not establish peak usage or
an OOM cause.

An explicit discovery inventory contains 1,447 unique methods. The interrupted
attempt completed 1,011 methods and started the next. A bounded continuation ran
the interrupted module and all remaining modules in 42 fresh module processes,
at most two concurrently. It completed 445 method invocations, including nine
explicit overlaps. Every module has a terminal footer and exact expected method
IDs/counts. Combined coverage accounts for all 1,447 unique IDs, with no missing
IDs. This is not a completed single-process full-suite pass.

Observed failures are retained and attributed with current-environment evidence:

   only repository-root paths. They were not repaired or rebaselined here
2. Two existing semantic-index tests initially error because the external guard
   blocks their writes of newly authored dummy `.env` fixtures. Those exact
   write-block errors also reproduce on clean `0efc2df`. A separately reviewed,
   two-method-only guard exception permits `.env` access solely inside a newly
