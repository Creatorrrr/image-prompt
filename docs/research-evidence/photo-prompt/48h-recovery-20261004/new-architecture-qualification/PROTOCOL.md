## Independent definition/effect comparison

Chapel's three unchanged component duties are intersecting STONE ribs across a CHAPEL ceiling, continuous ribs toward supports beside POINTED openings, and a distinct worship area at the CHAPEL END. The old Korean string supplies crossing ribs, wall-side continuity, and an end worship area, but omits stone, pointed openings and explicit chapel identity. Timber ribs or round-headed openings remain compatible with the old literal string while conflicting with hard selected duties. The proposed Korean wording supplies every missing selected fact and retains wall-side continuity. `상단이 뾰족한 개구부` denotes pointed openings without adding an era, place or cathedral-size claim. Retain it unchanged.

Arcade's three unchanged duties are an open lower arcade FACING WATER, a FINER OPENWORK GALLERY directly ABOVE it, and a BROAD UPPER WALL MASS above that gallery. The old Korean string omits water, finer openwork gallery material/form, and wall breadth. A dry inland plaza, solid upper gallery, or slender upper parapet may satisfy the old words. The proposal supplies those missing facts; distinct upper/lower opening rhythm and actual support are retained from the old Korean wording. The relative phrase `아래 아케이드보다 더 섬세한` is a conservative explicit reading of “finer,” not a claim that all Venetian architecture has this form. Retain the proposed wording unchanged. Source meanings do not establish named building, era, nationality, status, ownership, religion or identity.
