## Failure

- Conditions or trigger: Validate the first executable universal-candidate and compatibility drafts against the frozen goal and runtime design before copying them into the repository.
- Expected: near applies below 0.25, middle from 0.25 below 0.75, and far from 0.75; middle requires at least two distinct causal bridges; far requires entry, mediation, and exit bridges plus a preserved core anchor. Research packet references resolve to tracked manifest shard names.
- Observed: the draft widened the low band to 0.34, required only one bridge for middle and two for far, used `state` instead of the closed `state_change` bridge type, added noncanonical bridge labels outside the frozen seven-type enum, used noncanonical resource modes instead of `exclusive|shared`, recorded temporary packet filenames rather than tracked shard paths, omitted four embodiment profiles referenced by frozen scene contracts, and included forbidden pair-matrix/all-pairs solver keys even though their values were disabled.
- Later mutation finding: even after the compatibility asset declared the closed seven types, the runtime loader retained a broader `KNOWN_BRIDGE_TYPES` set. Adding `capability` to an executable candidate was accepted and changed bridge-count ranking. Rich research labels therefore still leaked into executable policy instead of remaining evidence-only mappings.
- Impact on the goal: high creativity could admit a remote premise with insufficient causal return, ordinary creativity could be routed to the wrong distance band, and provenance paths would not resolve. The draft cannot be ingested or used by the runtime.
