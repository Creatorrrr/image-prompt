- Expected: creativity changes the permitted distance distribution and bridge burden; seed and unseen identity can select among several coherent eligible event families without changing hard gates, candidate count, or the fixed core.

Other supporting atom IDs vary, but the coherent event spine is fixed by creativity band and does not form a distribution. Apple occupies two of three semantic families. Eleven context-distance profiles also apply distance and bridges through the same zero-distance `usl_core_identity_anchor`, so typed shape fingerprints can reproduce the original holdouts without proving unseen-scene generalization.
- Impact on the goal: the implementation encodes the demonstration examples rather than a concept-independent candidate distribution. It fails the goal's unseen-concept and creativity semantics even though the 24 construction fixtures pass.
