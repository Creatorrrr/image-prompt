## Failure

- Conditions or trigger: The user compared all four NEW candidates with the two earlier rejected images in the supplied contact sheet.
- Expected: At least one NEW candidate should be judged visibly more moe than both earlier images while remaining an attractive, cute adult character and respecting any case-specific tone constraint.

## Cause assessment

- Confirmed cause or current hypothesis: Confirmed evaluation confound and policy error, plus a provisional visual cause. The four candidates changed character identity as well as scene and moe mechanism, so their faces could not be compared as a controlled variable. The implementation also misread “moe can be nonsexual” as “moe should default to nonsexual,” suppressing a low-intensity adult-appeal axis that the user says can help. The prompts spent substantial visual bandwidth on authored props and event-state proof; a textual `pretty-and-cute` entry condition did not guarantee a face and micro-expression the user would experience as a cute bishoujo.
- Confidence: mixed: the failed user outcome and identity confound are confirmed; the relative contribution of face appeal, expression, styling, and event complexity remains a hypothesis.
- Remaining unknowns: Whether a bounded revision can preserve the fixed-identity v3 gaze, tsundere tension, and user-approved low-intensity adult sensual cue while making concealed liking subtly visible without becoming overt.
