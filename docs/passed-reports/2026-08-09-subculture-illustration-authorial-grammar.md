## Successful approach

- Freeze before implementation: bind 24 natural requests and six render cases to topic, format, required pixel focus, thumbnail/crop/sequence checks, forbidden convergence, and a bounded image budget.
- Separate evidence roles: keep source-supported mechanisms, cross-source synthesis, design inference, visual atoms, routers, and guards distinct. Never turn a market term, national label, or viewer response claim into pixel evidence.
- Use one sparse grammar: select one primary visual atom and no more than two compatible supports. Compose focal hierarchy, controlled omission, edge/mark behavior, and one state-changing motif as observable decisions.
- Treat authorial touch as repeatable choice: use a familiar anchor, one changed rule, one causal first-to-second-look reveal, two visible consequences, and format-native framing rather than a named style or unusual noun.
- Bind renderability before generation: every high-creativity v2 prompt names a primary carrier, a distinct safe fallback, protected loci, exact consequences, risk flags, and the inspection scales where pixels must pass.
- Qualify pixels separately: preserve each attempt, inspect without prompt metadata, allow one cause-specific fallback only after primary failure, and never generate a batch to select the most favorable image.

## Reuse guidance

- Prefer: a first-read event plus one isolated second-look relation built from several independent physical facts, protected by negative space and checked at declared delivery scales.
- Avoid: strengthening an ambiguous cue with adjectives, using compound anatomy or tiny glyphs as the sole reveal, accepting a thin line as a broad boundary, or accepting substrate-aligned color as a state change.
- Preserve: failed images, exact prompts, native paths, hashes, attempted roles, review scales, and the condition that a fallback is unused when the primary passes.
- Re-check when: the image model, carrier type, target scale, format profile, candidate graph, audit schema, or frozen pixel rubric changes.
