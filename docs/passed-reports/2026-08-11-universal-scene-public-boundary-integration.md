## Retained limitations

- Public 24-case text/runtime qualification does not prove behavior on hidden or unseen contracts.
- Static 12-profile inventory does not replace the excluded 12×3×32 entropy, modal-share, cross-contract, and two-fresh-process qualification.
- Prompt composition and pixel-evidence contracts do not prove actual generated-image legibility; no image was generated or reviewed for this goal.
- These limits are intentional scope boundaries, not implied passes. Reopen the generalization report before making either unseen-generalization or rendered-pixel claims.

## Reuse guidance

- Prefer: source-role separation, literal-bound current authority, one bounded event spine, exact source-owner replay, independent audit recomputation, and explicit nonclaims when a broader qualification is skipped.
- Minimum verification when reused: raw hash-chain load, public current-oracle projection, all affected deterministic packs, compiled obligations, composition carriers, relevant legacy/photo replay, and focused source-authority mutations.
- Applicable when: adding concept-independent scene atoms or semantic bindings without changing the hidden/generalization or actual-image claim boundary.
- Do not apply when: claiming unseen-contract diversity, cross-process invariance, or rendered-pixel quality; those require their own frozen evidence and reopening the open report.
- Re-check or invalidate when: research manifest, candidate/compatibility/semantic bytes, current oracle lineage, role/resource/bridge schemas, composition carrier rules, or Python serialization behavior changes.
