# Composition Contract

Post-core only. This reference must not participate in initial meaning resolution or baseline authoring. Follow `SKILL.md` for user authority, retry-only parent access, and the compact composition view. Full source packs remain the audit input; a view never replaces them.

## Candidate Pack v6: Normal Skill Contract

The normal skill path explicitly requests `--candidate-pack-version v6` and supplies one external `photo-request-envelope/v1` plus one `photo-authorial-core/v3`. The raw CLI still defaults to v4 for compatibility, so omission of the version flag is not a valid normal-skill shortcut. V5/v2 and v1 cores remain compatibility or historical-audit paths.

The request envelope is frozen before delegation. It contains the complete byte-exact requester text, its SHA-256, and one or more exact non-overlapping active spans. Multi-topic runs keep the same raw requester text and select only exact arm-local topic and governing global-modifier spans; an agent-authored per-arm paraphrase is invalid. The agent writes an initial core before reading any pack, profile, registry, project reference, fixture, or prior result. A retry may inspect only the parent hashes, preserved frozen fields, effective hard obligations, and relevant defect evidence allowed by `SKILL.md`; previous candidate inventories and optional prose remain unavailable. The core contains that same complete raw text, a context-resolved interpretation, concrete subject/setting/event, two to six visual priorities, a standalone 48–640-word English baseline prompt with a default recommended maximum of 360 words, optional request-scoped definitions, true requester exclusions, optional runtime-only labels, optional style evidence, `interpretation_provenance`, mandatory empty `unresolved_ambiguities`, and an `agent_prepack` provenance marker. New v5/v6 packs expose `photo-authorial-prompt-budget/v2`: 48 and 640 are blocking bounds, 360 is advisory, and required literal evidence expands the advisory ceiling by a 160-word headroom. Recorded `photo-authorial-prompt-budget/v1` packs retain their 24–320 blocking bounds, 180-word advisory ceiling, and 80-word headroom; packs created before any prompt-budget sub-contract retain the historical 24–180 hard boundary when replayed.

`photo-intent-lock/v1` makes requesting-user meaning the highest composition priority. It requires semantic anchors whose substantive source text is grounded in active requester spans and whose positive evidence is literal in the baseline. Every active span and every locked dimension has anchor coverage, and every locked dimension receives a distinct evidence phrase rather than reusing one generic phrase; `concept`, `subject`, and `event` are always locked, and every other requester-specified semantic dimension must also be locked. Open and locked dimensions are disjoint. A v3 core may have zero or one open dimension; do not invent freedom for a precise request or repair. Every active span additionally has either a real requester definition or typed interpretation provenance. A requester definition's source must equal a complete active span and cannot be inferred from a bare term; bare or externally interpreted terms use `interpretation_provenance`. The generator rejects unresolved ambiguity, unknown or pack-derived fields, ungrounded definitions/exclusions/labels, incomplete span or dimension coverage, and any source bytes that differ from the envelope, then canonicalizes and hash-binds the envelope, request binding, intent lock, and core.

New v6 packs include `authorial_composition.authorship_policy` with `contract_version: photo-authorial-authorship-policy/v1`, the source core and intent-lock hashes, `allowed_dimensions` copied from the frozen open dimensions, and `minimum_authorial_decisions: min(2, len(open_dimensions))`. Each decision remains substantive and uses a distinct allowed dimension. Zero open dimensions requires explicit `open_dimensions: []` in the core and `authorial_decisions: []` in the composed binding; one open dimension requires one decision. All anchors and the three preserved baseline phrases remain mandatory. The pack's `core_binding_contract` uses `photo-authorial-core-binding/v2` and binds `source_authorship_policy_sha256`; this is pack metadata, not an extra composer-authored field. The auditor independently recomputes the policy and binding contract. V2–v5 packs cannot opt in, and serialized packs without the policy retain the legacy minimum of two decisions.

Semantic retrieval uses the core, not a broad default intent. The positive query combines the exact active requester spans after explicit-exclusion redaction, interpreted meaning, subject, setting, event, priorities, baseline, requester-definition expansion, interpretation resolutions, and optional style evidence. Research URLs do not enter the query. `user_exclusions` are only true requester negatives and never become positive search text. `runtime_forbidden_labels` remain positive retrieval/profile evidence while their literal spellings are prohibited only in the baseline/final/runtime prompt. Mandatory-intent audit uses the lock's positive anchor evidence rather than naively promoting every natural-language token, so a negation or connective cannot be inverted into a positive duty. `provenance.retrieval_query` records the core, envelope, active-scope, and intent-lock hashes plus contributing/redacted fields and recomputable query hash; the composed audit rejects mutation.

V5 separates meaning from exploration:

- `photo-semantic-clarification/v1` is deterministic across creativity and seed. It exposes a v2/v3 core interpretation as required and non-revisable, preserves requester definitions as immutable required evidence, and exposes matched visual-profile meanings with `required`, `eligible`, `context_mismatch`, or `requires_existing_adult_context` applicability. Reject a mismatched optional row and continue. A material correction to the governing meaning requires a rebuilt envelope/core/pack; use a clear requester correction already supplied, and ask only when the meaning is actually unresolved. It does not sample or add a new adult/safety route.
- `photo-creative-augmentation/v1` ranks the same hard-eligible advisory pool for a fixed core and generation seed, partitions it by relative relevance into `near`, `adjacent`, and `lateral`, then samples without replacement. Requested creativity permits near only at `0..0.25`, near plus adjacent below `0.75`, and all three bands from `0.75..1`. Hard guards, negative bytes, explicit exclusions, identity/species/no-people, safety, and conflicts are invariant. Every transformed decision names distinct `affected_dimensions`, all of which must be open and subordinate to the intent lock.

`photo-downstream-intent-precedence/v1` applies the same boundary to non-candidate defaults. Each downstream rule declares its affected dimensions and is active only when all are explicitly open. Suppressed rules cannot leave a positive instruction, negative-prompt term, composed evidence duty, or render gate behind. Locked-dimension evidence reuses the matching semantic anchor exactly; other non-open evidence must already be literal in the frozen baseline. The audit recomputes this projection from the core, so removing the metadata cannot bypass it. This is topic-neutral: do not add named-archetype or named-expression exceptions.

`photo-negative-intent-guard/v1` applies a second topic-neutral boundary to both prompt surfaces. Modern cores reject instruction-shaped blanket negatives in `baseline_prompt_en`, and composed audit rejects their reintroduction into `prompt_en`. Automatic `negative_en` is restricted to the maintained intent-neutral photographic-defect vocabulary; a semantic term is admitted only when, after removing a literal directive prefix such as `no`, it equals one complete true active-scope requester exclusion. Substring splitting, synonym expansion, and category broadening are forbidden, while identity-preservation controls require explicit identity-reference mode. Generic safety, taste, count, contact, action, relationship, expression, wardrobe, and genre suppressions are not automatic prompt material. Platform/tool policy remains enforced outside the prompt, and a necessary local boundary is written as positive geometry or visible state. The public guard binds the core hash, intent-lock hash, identity mode, exact requester exclusions, emitted terms, and fixed policies with a canonical hash; the composed auditor recomputes the entire object rather than trusting it.

Visual-profile clarification uses one registry-bound hybrid resolver after the core exists. Its generated sidecar contains exact lookup rows and semantic vectors derived from the same authored registry. Boundary-aware exact request hits may project as existing hard visual obligations. Embedding-only hits may project only as optional visual concepts and eligible clarifications; they cannot add prompt evidence or render gates until explicitly selected. The resolver is computed once and shared by all three visual-profile projections. Exact, negated, context-mismatched, and requester-defined meanings cannot be resurrected by similarity. The pack exposes neither vectors, scores, matched terms, match basis, nor rank.

The public v5 pack contains no relevance score, probability, sampler winner, fixed three-route hybrid answer, or candidate prose. Every creative row contains unordered concept terms and a semantic band only. A seed chooses material within the allowed range; array order is non-preferential. General candidate retrieval may use rule/context fallback for offline inspection, but v5 visual-profile rule mode is exact-only; only semantic-mode evidence proves embedding-based profile discovery. V4 retains its prior lexical optional-profile behavior for compatibility.

The composed object keeps the three v5 bindings and adds typed v6 bindings:

- `authorial_core_binding` repeats the exact core and intent-lock hashes, preserves every anchor ID and anchor evidence, lists at least three distinct substantive phrases occurring literally in both baseline and final prompt, and records the number of substantive authored decisions required by the pack's authorship policy, each with a distinct open dimension and rationale. Without that policy the legacy minimum remains two. Every explicit `user_exclusions` and `runtime_forbidden_labels` phrase remains absent from the final prompt.
- `negative_intent_guard` is pack-owned and has no composer override. `negative_en` remains byte-identical to the pack, but equality alone is insufficient: every emitted item must be an intent-neutral defect, an exact requester-grounded exclusion, or an enabled identity-preservation control, and neither baseline nor final positive prompt may contain a blanket negative directive.
- `semantic_clarification_decisions` gives every clarification one typed decision. Ordinary rows use `applied` or `rejected`: required meanings cannot be rejected, and context-mismatched or adult-context-gated meanings cannot be applied. V2/v3 core and requester-definition rows are never revisable. `superseded_by_revision` remains auditable only on a historical v1 core hypothesis; it is invalid for v2/v3. Applied evidence is literal, requester evidence is preserved exactly, and runtime-only labels stay absent.
- `creative_augmentation_brief.decisions` decides every sampled creative row as `transformed` or `rejected`. At most three may be transformed. A transformed ID enters `chosen_candidate_ids` and receives interpretation, transformation, rationale, newly authored literal evidence with at least two content words beyond its source terms, and open-only `affected_dimensions`. Rejecting every creative row is valid.
- If `semantic_assertion_obligations` exists, `semantic_assertion_evidence` contains exactly `source_contract_sha256` plus an `evidence` map keyed by every required non-character assertion ID. Fields and phrases byte-equal the frozen core evidence and every phrase is literal in `prompt_en`; omitting the pack block, assertion, field, or phrase fails closed.
- If `render_repair` exists, `render_repair_evidence` contains exactly its canonical hash plus an evidence map keyed by every lineage repair ID. The interaction and recognition phrases byte-equal the frozen pre-retrieval core, occur literally in `prompt_en`, and are already owned by one required action assertion. The contract is object-agnostic: it preserves actor, object class, interaction state, and required or transitional contact while permitting only declared local repair axes. `relation_origin: parent_preserved` protects an unchanged parent relation; `requester_corrected` freezes an explicit requester correction after the new core is built. Removal, relocation, concealment, or transfer never counts as fidelity repair.
- If `character_response` exists, its specialized composed binding remains authoritative for the one required causal response assertion. The audit also compares its assertion ID, source spans, axes, and frozen evidence directly with the core, so removing or mutating the compiled contract cannot bypass the typed meaning.

V5 preserves the v4 authorial scene, open-slot, visual-obligation, viewer, moe, creative-direction, adult-axis, negative, and ordinary candidate-interpretation contracts where present, but all of them are subordinate to `intent_preservation`. They may realize or enrich open dimensions and cannot replace a locked requester meaning. It moves an allowed adult-axis block to top-level `adult_appeal` and expects `adult_appeal_brief`; a skill-default adult axis is omitted unless sexual tone, style, composition, expression, pose, body geometry, framing, and lighting are all explicitly open. Explicit requester controls remain subject to the frozen core and ordinary safety checks.

## Candidate Pack v4

`--emit-candidate-pack` returns a JSON array. Generate and audit one pack at a time. The default pack carries `contract_version: photo-candidate-pack/v4`; `pack_id` is the first 16 hexadecimal characters of SHA-256 over canonical JSON with `pack_id` replaced by `null`.

v4 makes the agent the final author rather than a phrase assembler. Candidate labels, long candidate terms, photographic suggestion phrases, craft prompt fragments, and render-blueprint prose are not exposed as reusable final wording. Candidate surfaces contain unordered `concept_terms` and no sampler-selected flag, probability, weight, score, slot-level selected answer, or candidate-ID `covered_by` answer key. Candidate and route order is seed-shuffled and non-preferential: use individual technical, identity, or material terms selectively, but invent their relationship, causality, timing, and final prose. `authorial_composition.variation_lens` is a seed-varying abstract lens, not a mandatory phrase or scene.

v3 remains a replay projection for the immediately prior literal-blueprint contract, and v2 preserves its older public bookkeeping and profile aliases. Use `--candidate-pack-version v3` or `v2` only for a legacy consumer or historical reproduction and supply `--legacy-replay-reason`; the pack records that reason. An explicit seed keeps those projections reproducible; an omitted seed now receives and records a fresh random effective seed.

For independent variations, broad aesthetics, and Japanese-subculture photo requests, create `authorial-request/v1` before pack generation and pass it with `--authorial-request-json`. Its canonical SHA-256 binds an agent-authored subject, setting, unfinished event, typed style family, at least two visible style-evidence phrases, and an arm-local variation key. It rejects pack-derived keys. In v4, this public pre-pack request governs `authorial_scene`; the composer records the same SHA under `authored_scene.source_authorial_request_sha256`. The audit checks canonical integrity and subject/setting/event preservation. A candidate pack may inform optional detail transformations only after this request is frozen.

`photo-visual-intent/v1` is a separate post-core, pre-pack input for non-substitutable visual meaning. Use it only after the baseline hash exists and only when the request explicitly defines exact pose geometry, physical support, simultaneous expression components, body-region salience, an embodied state transition, or a behavioral archetype. A row may omit `profile_id`; the generator then resolves exactly one direct registry profile from its exact source text, failing closed on zero or multiple matches. An explicit ID remains supported for post-core maintenance and replay. A v4 or v5 pack hashes that input into `photo-visual-obligations/v1`; it never converts these hard duties into optional candidate IDs. Related objects, generic styling, reference appearance, or excluded homonyms cannot hard-activate a profile. Strong indirect component combinations and weaker nearby cues instead create unordered, non-ranked `photo-visual-concepts/v1` candidates with no matched terms, score, or routing reason. The composed object must include `chosen_visual_concept_ids` when such candidates exist, even if empty. Selecting one promotes its immutable opt-in obligation to the effective hard contract; not selecting it adds no prompt or review duty. A requester definition has precedence and remains local to that request. If an activation label is unsuitable for the runtime prompt, use `coverage_assertions` to bind its exact mandatory-intent key to one safe component phrase; definition-only runtime profiles still require every other component independently.

`japanese-subculture-photo/v1` selects one concrete family from the existing scoped subculture taxonomy and requires at least two literal visible cues. A style label alone fails, as does converting the style request into Japanese nationality, ethnicity, or facial morphology. Strong-theme candidates named by its guard stay absent unless the original request explicitly names them. Prompt audit proves literal binding only; pixel survival is reviewed separately.

`--explain-scene-routing` publishes a diagnostic-only private trace of boundary-aware `selection_cues`, scores, and the selected blueprint ID. Do not compose from that output. Ordinary v4 packs keep the trace absent. A blueprint marked `requires_selection_cue` is ineligible for a no-evidence seed fallback; explicit boundary-aware cues can still select it.

v4 keeps only reproducibility, user-input, reference-control, and safety provenance. It withholds expanded argv, the private preset ID, sample-prompt hash, internal concept routes, duplicated character/adult routing, singleton preset candidates, and sampler-selected motifs. Its public `quality_profile` carries only request-critical subject kind; photographic integration categories and craft dimensions are unordered optional pools with no selected axis, strategy, refinement, score, or matched-facet answer key.

Sampler-built atomic scene groups are also private routing in v4. Their public projection is an `optional_inspiration_group` containing only non-ranked `allowed_candidate_ids`; the group name, selected entry, semantic entry-ID lists, scene-tag winner, and required-adoption rule are absent. Every group and every member may be rejected. v3/v2 retain the fail-closed atomic scene and selected-entry bookkeeping for replay.

If v4 would otherwise expose only one subject/action/location/prop candidate, it removes that singleton from `slots` and declares an `authorial_open_slots` entry instead. The composer supplies `authored_slots.<slot>.prompt_evidence` and `artistic_rationale`; a role-bound location also acknowledges its abstract `scene_family`. This preserves identity, no-people, safety, and role-family constraints without turning one sampled scene into the only permissible prose. When a private render blueprint exists, the broader `authored_scene` contract owns all four atoms instead.

The audit rejects a changed pack, a list containing zero or multiple packs, a missing composed field, a non-agent composer, an empty required candidate selection, a changed negative prompt, failed concept gates, and a non-pass safety record. Optional candidates may all be rejected.

Candidate caps are four presets, four candidates for core slots, two for support slots, and 64 slot candidates in total. Every sampled selection is reserved before alternatives so truncation cannot silently remove it. Ordinary slot alternatives come from the exact pool recorded after sampler applicability, no-people, compatibility, and hard-conflict filtering, with `applicability.source: sampler_eligible_pool`.

A resolved render blueprint remains private source material in v4. The public pack exposes only abstract scene functions, relationship stakes, genre anchors, visual-evidence types, and diegetic provenance. It never exposes the source subject/action/location/prop prose, blueprint ID, hash, or sibling inventory. The composer supplies a new `authored_scene` with a governing premise, artistic rationale, four newly written prompt-bound atoms, and at least two distinct interpretive choices. v3/v2 retain the prior four-literal-atom behavior solely for replay compatibility.

An explicit `--creativity` value from `0.75` through `1.0` may add `creative_exploration`. Its contrast rows never add, remove, or reorder candidates: each `candidate_id` is an already exposed `sampler_eligible_pool` option that clears conflicts and exceeds the declared feature-distance floor. v4 removes the prior `replaces_candidate_id` and relevance-rank answer key; use no more than `composition_guidance.use_at_most` mutually compatible options by artistic judgment. v3/v2 retain the replacement bookkeeping for replay. The field is absent below the activation floor.

The same explicit range adds `creative_direction`, an agent-level creation and selection contract with no topic examples or new candidates. It requires an ordinary-baseline critique, at least four proposals using distinct concept operators, exactly one selection, one rule change, at least two visible consequences, a staged reveal path, and a concrete authorial grammar. Its evidence fields must be literal in `prompt_en`; signatures from unselected proposals are forbidden. See `creative-direction-contract.md`. The field is absent below the activation floor, so ordinary composed prompts retain their existing contract.

`--hybrid-augmentation`, high creative direction, or an active adult-appeal axis adds `hybrid_augmentation`. In v4 it exposes three seed-rotated two-to-four-detail routes assembled from actual candidate IDs, but each detail carries only unordered concept terms. The composer may reject every route. When selecting one, every detail is `transformed` or `rejected`; one to three transformed IDs enter `chosen_candidate_ids` with an artistic interpretation, transformation description, transformation dimensions, marginal value, and newly authored literal evidence. Evidence made only by joining source terms fails audit. Active adult-appeal axes require an agent-authored axis interpretation, not adoption of a fixed inventory phrase. See `hybrid-augmentation-contract.md`. v3/v2 retain `accepted|modified|rejected` for replay.

All advisory v4 candidates are optional, including optional scene-group, visual-proposition, photographic-integration, and photographic-craft candidates. Hard identity, species-family, enforced role-scene, and safety constraints remain required when declared. For every chosen non-augmentation ID, `candidate_interpretations` contains exactly one row with an artistic interpretation, a transformation description, and literal newly authored prompt evidence. The evidence has at least four content words and introduces at least two content words beyond the source concept terms. An empty `chosen_candidate_ids` list is valid only when the authored request and hard contracts need no candidate.

`--viewer-experience` adds the separate topic-neutral `photo-viewer-experience/v1` composition contract. High creative-direction runs include it automatically. The contract changes neither presets nor slots; it requires one viewing context, audience-literacy scope, primary viewer need, scalar intended experience, visible first-glance hook, actor/action/target/consequence, attachment and reinspection conditions, and one commercial objective. Only declared visible evidence is literal in `prompt_en`; the intended response remains a hypothesis. See `viewer-experience-contract.md`. Ordinary requests without an explicit audience, affect, attachment, commercial, subculture-response, or creative goal retain the previous pack shape.

An explicit natural-language character-moe request adds `moe_response_contract/v10` and automatically enables viewer experience. On legacy or explicitly open dimensions it can require the routed adult aesthetic baseline, relationship register, causal mechanism, warm affective leak, and plain-background control described in `moe-response-contract.md`. On v5, however, only fields actually present in `prompt_binding.required_evidence_fields` are duties: `intent_precedence` removes any generic requirement whose affected dimensions are not all open. A locked expression therefore cannot acquire a smile, softened eyes, warmth, recovery beat, or negative-affect cap unless that evidence already belongs to the frozen requester meaning. A locked style cannot acquire a generic pretty/cute styling prescription, and a closed text or sexual-tone dimension cannot acquire the plain-background or sensual-support default. Exact request-derived visual obligations remain hard, but they must realize the same anchors rather than revise them. Identity-controlled prompts still preserve eye aperture/shape/spacing, face length, lower-face/jaw width, and adult age with explicit anti-reshape clauses. In new packs, the final English `prompt_en` prefers 100–240 words, but that compact range is advisory under the absolute 48–640 bounds. Serialized packs retain their recorded prompt-budget values, including the former `minimum_words`/`maximum_words` hard-range shape. See `moe-response-contract.md`.

## Required Composition Behavior

- When `contract_version` is v5/v6, validate `request_binding`, `authorial_core`, `intent_lock`, complete active-span/locked-dimension coverage, interpretation provenance and empty unresolved list, recomputed retrieval provenance, `intent_preservation`, `authorial_core_binding`, every semantic-clarification decision, and every creative-augmentation decision. V6 also validates its typed assertion contracts and authorship policy when present. Candidate choice never outranks the frozen core. A material meaning change requires a rebuilt run; rejecting an optional mismatch does not.
- Use every `mandatory_intents[].text` as literal or faithfully translated visible content.
- `mandatory_intents` contains required positive user meaning. Read `intent_contract` for the complete typed request: `user_requirement` and `additional_requirement` are required, `role_requirement` and `soft_guidance` are advisory, and `negative_requirement` is excluded.
- `audit_terms` and candidate labels are discovery aids, not proof of user-intent coverage.
- `coverage_assertions` may map an exact mandatory-intent key to one or more phrases. Every phrase must occur literally in `prompt_en`.
- Choose IDs only from `presets`, `slots`, proposition candidates, or the adult-appeal candidate inventory included in the pack.
- Choose only candidates with `applicability.status: eligible`.
- Do not choose both sides of a hard conflict.
- Preserve `negative_en` exactly, including `null`.
- Set `composer` to `agent`.
- When `viewer_experience.enabled` is true, add the composed `viewer_experience` object and obey its conditional evidence rules.
- When `moe_response.enabled` is true, add the composed `moe_response` object and bind exactly the fields in `prompt_binding.required_evidence_fields`. Preserve explicit routed mechanisms, but when `intent_precedence` suppresses a generic mechanism, use the frozen event/expression anchors instead. `affective_leak_phrase`, `aesthetic_baseline_phrase`, causal-chain fields, and `background_control_phrase` are conditional rather than universal.
- When `visual_obligations.enabled` is true, add `visual_obligation_evidence.<profile_id>` for exactly the active profiles and fields. Every field is a distinct literal substring of `prompt_en`; any pre-pack `bindings` value is preserved exactly. The ordered union of `render_gates[].id` is authoritative and is merged into `moe_response.render_qualification.required_hard_gates`.
- When `visual_concept_candidates.enabled` is true, add `chosen_visual_concept_ids`, even when empty. For every selected ID, treat `opt_in_contract.obligation` exactly like an active hard obligation: supply its semantic evidence fields, obey its `runtime_expression.default_mode`, and include its gates in the derived effective checklist. The pack remains immutable; audits derive the effective contract from pack plus composed selection instead of trusting a review's self-reported gate list.
- When `moe_response.reference_identity_control.enabled` is true, also bind `reference_identity_phrase`: name the uploaded portrait as the sole identity source, preserve at least three listed face anchors plus adult age, and forbid face substitution, structural beautification, and de-aging. Let expression, pose, outfit, light, and setting carry the tested change.
- When v4 `hybrid_augmentation.enabled` is true, add `augmentation_brief`, preserve its transformation budget, and bind every transformed detail with newly authored literal evidence. For v3/v2 replay, bind accepted or modified details under the legacy contract.
- When v5 `creative_augmentation.enabled` is true, add `creative_augmentation_brief`, decide every sampled ID, choose only transformed IDs, and keep the maximum of three. Every transformed row declares distinct `affected_dimensions` contained in `intent_lock.open_dimensions`; rejected IDs stay absent and source-term-only evidence fails.
- When v5/v6 `semantic_clarification.enabled` is true, add exactly one decision per clarification, apply every required meaning, reject inapplicable senses, and bind component evidence without runtime-only labels. Do not supersede a v2/v3 core.
- When `authorial_open_slots` is present, add one `authored_slots` decision per listed slot with newly written literal evidence and an artistic rationale. A role-scene family named in the slot constraints must appear in `constraint_acknowledgments` as a contract acknowledgment, not prompt prose.
- When `authorial_request` is present, copy its canonical SHA into `authored_scene.source_authorial_request_sha256`, preserve its subject/setting/event semantics in the corresponding atoms and prompt, and bind its typed style evidence. Never write this object after seeing the pack.
- When `japanese_subculture_photo` is present, use the typed family, bind at least two listed visible cues, keep the design original and unbranded, and avoid nationality, ethnicity, or facial-feature inference.
- For each ordinary v4 `chosen_candidate_ids` entry, add one `candidate_interpretations` row. Do not add rows for rejected candidates or for transformed augmentation details already documented in `augmentation_brief`.

## Masking and Generalization

Semantic dropout removes masked slot candidates, selected IDs, labels, terms, and preset values from the public pack. `open_slots` contains only the slot name, bucket, status, and reason. Fill the opening from the user request and remaining contract; never reconstruct the hidden exemplar.

If a composed ID names an open slot, the audit fails. Masked details are also removed from conflicts and mandatory-intent coverage metadata.

## Meaning and Coherence

- `intent_contract` and `coverage.intent_constraints`: typed source, polarity, subject category or exact curated subject route, domain, negative-presence constraint, and matching evidence. Excluded and advisory rows constrain or guide composition but are not positive mandatory prose.
- `scene_contract`: v4 candidate-backed groups are optional non-ranked inspiration pools with no sampler winner; they never require adoption. A v4 `authorial_scene` group instead requires newly authored subject, action, location, and prop evidence plus explicit interpretive judgment and contains no source prose. v3/v2 `atomic_scene` and `selected_render_blueprint` groups keep their fail-closed selected-entry and literal-label behavior for replay.
- `render_contract.selected_scene`: one selected scene function set, one diegetic visual provenance, relationship stakes, genre anchors, and visual evidence types. v4 exposes an authoring contract, not a source hash, blueprint ID, or atomic prose. Selection bookkeeping, source text, and available-blueprint inventories remain internal.
- `character_grammar`: an on-demand character route exposes one to three selected `visual_atom` nodes, one marked primary, plus visual evidence types and generic composition constraints. In v4, use these definitions and the abstract scene dimensions to invent the visible realization. Keep exactly one primary mechanism and at most two support cues. Runtime router anchors, compatibility-edge IDs, policy/guard records, identity, orientation, and adulthood policy are validated before the public projection and are never visual costume or morphology instructions. v3/v4 omit domain/topic/family IDs; v2 restores them only for compatibility.
- `--scene-function` is an optional control for direct taxonomy presets. It requires `--preset`, does not add a mandatory intent, and fails closed for an unknown or unavailable function. A no-people request first removes every blueprint that is not explicitly declared non-human; an empty or human-only remainder is an error rather than a silent human render.
- `evidence_budget`: count chosen slot names, not candidate count. A materialized scene prop may be the first physical clue; when the range is 1–2, choose no more than one additional listed clue slot.
- `concept_axes.required`: show each meaning axis through behavior, placement, expression, material, light, or framing.
- `role_scene_policy`: when `enforce` is true, select an allowed location ID; omission is a failure.
- `species_family`: select every required family slot from the allowed family. Missing IDs and mismatches both fail.
- `motif_budget.discouraged_now`: avoid repeated motifs unless the user explicitly requested one.
- `concept_gates`: machine-evaluable gates must be `pass`; the wrapper blocks failures before pack generation. A gate declared `manual` requires literal `manual_gate_evidence` with `review_stage: pixel_review_required`. ## Safety

Default safety uses the pack's ordinary report mode and adds no approval wait. Run `--safety-evaluation` only when the user explicitly asks for a safety review. It changes only the report mode and lists evaluated recipe transforms; it does not grant extra authority and does not replace platform or image-tool policy. Safety evaluation and platform enforcement must not manufacture prompt semantics: unless the requester explicitly excluded an element, do not convert a safety transform into `No ...`, `never ...`, or an automatic `negative_en` term. Prefer a narrowly positive visible state when one is needed, and leave non-prompt enforcement to the tool and pixel-review boundary.

Adult-appeal axes are composition inputs, not safety transforms. Legacy eligible-human packs use the configured `sensual_editorial=1`, `fetish_fashion=0` default; v5 emits that default only across explicitly open dimensions. Explicit controls may override or disable it while remaining bound by the frozen requester core. Permit `sensual_editorial` and `fetish_fashion` simultaneously when explicitly activated, but require an explicitly adult original subject, visible agency, and combined styling-pose-framing-camera review whenever active. ## Optional Candidate Semantic Units and Bundles

Modern v6 packs may declare `candidate_semantic_surface_version: photo-candidate-semantic-surface/v1`. Their unordered `concept_units` preserve short internal relationships; `concept_terms` carries the same units for the ordinary authorship audit. Interpret these units in a newly authored scene. `candidate_bundles.candidates` are optional complete realizations. Read the full detail view before adopting one. Select its `bundle:*` ID and provide ordinary artistic interpretation/transformation evidence plus `component_evidence` for every component ID and `relation_evidence` for every relation ID. Its `associated_profile_ids` remain advisory and never automatically activate visual profiles.

Some bundles satisfy member prerequisites only through joint adoption. Their nested members do not become standalone choices unless those candidates are independently present and eligible in the ordinary pack. The generator and auditor recompute joint admission from the frozen core and the same skill source snapshot; unsupported external context, unknown dimension ownership, locked dimensions, conflicts, or modified source contracts fail closed. Bundle selection requires prompt-level evidence; it is not a new claim of successful pixels or user acceptance. Earlier serialized v4/v5/v6 surfaces retain their original reading contracts.
