# Frozen blind prompt rubric

Score each anonymous English prompt without seeing its render, method, candidate data, or mapping.

- Requested concept fidelity (30): unmistakably adult nurse, visible handheld syringe, and a yandere meaning expressed as target-specific affection plus possessive/control behavior rather than label, costume, gaze, or threat alone.
- Visible single-frame causality (25): one identifiable target, a concrete target-directed action, an affect leak, and an already-visible consequence can plausibly appear together.
- Face-reference and freedom constraint (10): the supplied image is limited to facial appearance while pose, setting, wardrobe, and composition remain newly authored.
- Photographic direction (15): coherent setting, framing, lighting, hierarchy, and material detail rather than a tag bag.
- Runtime usability (10): clear, non-contradictory instructions that an image model can execute.
- Economy and authorship (10): concise standalone prose with no redundant taxonomy labels or instruction-shaped blanket negatives.

Give a 0–100 score, evidence for every category, a preference, confidence, and any likely pixel-delivery risk. A tie is allowed.
