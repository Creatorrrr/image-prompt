# Frozen blind pixel rubric

Score each anonymous render from pixels only, first at thumbnail scale and then at native size. Do not inspect prompts, generation method, sibling directories, or mapping.

- Facial-appearance fidelity to the supplied reference (15): recognizable adult facial proportions, eye character, delicate lower face, lips, and pale ash-blonde hair; do not reward copied pose, clothing, or background.
- Adult nurse role and syringe clarity (15): unmistakably adult nurse and an intelligible handheld syringe.
- Yandere relational legibility (30): visible affection or care directed at one identifiable target, visibly possessive or access-controlling behavior toward that same target, and a consequence already readable in-frame. A pretty, frightening, jealous, or syringe-holding nurse alone earns little here.
- Single-frame readability (10): the action, target, emotional contradiction, and consequence are understandable without a caption.
- Photographic craft (20): coherent composition, lighting, depth, texture, color, and intentional visual hierarchy.
- Technical coherence (10): hands, eyes, syringe, cables/props, clothing, and spatial relations are plausible and free of distracting generation artifacts.

Also record pass/fail for: adult actor; nurse readable; syringe readable; yandere readable at thumbnail; reference limited to face rather than copied composition. Give a concise evidence-based preference and confidence. A tie is allowed.
