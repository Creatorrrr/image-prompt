A hurried, unposed street photograph at a rain-soaked bus stop late at night. One unmistakably adult person stands alone on the pavement beside the shelter, gripping a small stack of wet paper documents in their left hand at waist level, with that elbow loosely bent; the pages sag, stick together, and drip onto the ground. Their feet stay planted beneath their weight and their free arm hangs beside their coat. Their shoulders are slumped, their chin lowered, and tired eyes follow the departing vehicle. The final bus is already pulling away from the stop in the same frame, its rear doors shut and red taillights receding along the empty road. The widening gap between the stranded adult and the moving bus makes the missed ride clear at a glance. Shot by a passerby while moving past, at human height with a slightly tilted frame and a trace of motion blur, using available bus-stop light reflected in wet asphalt. The adult fills the near left third while the bus remains readable in the right middle distance, their separation traced by the rain-glossed curb line. An amber streetlamp above the shelter skims the wet pages and one side of the adult's face, its warm pool reflected on the asphalt.
