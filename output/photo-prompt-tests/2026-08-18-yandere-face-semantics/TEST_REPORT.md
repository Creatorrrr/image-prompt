## Implemented contract

The durable same-target relation remains unchanged: one adult affection target, sincere affection, a concrete boundary/access action, and one visible consequence for that same target. Facial conventions are supporting evidence and cannot activate or prove the archetype alone.

Five mutually exclusive primary face modes were added:

1. sweet-threat mismatch;
2. dead-eye devotion;
3. ecstatic face cradle;
4. manic possessiveness;
5. abandonment fracture.

The contract treats pupil scale, iris scale, catchlight state, iris contrast, and gaze focus as independent controls. A vacant gaze is not target lock, pupil size is not proof of affection, and a photorealistic eye must retain scene-coherent catchlights. Generic portraits containing dead eyes, tiny pupils, or a cheek-cradle pose do not hard-activate the yandere relation.
