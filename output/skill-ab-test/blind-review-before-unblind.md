# Blind pixel review before condition reveal

Both 1086x1448 outputs were copied to `blind-1.png` and `blind-2.png` using an unobserved random coin flip. Prompts, audit records, and condition folders were not opened before these scores were fixed. Review was performed at 256 px thumbnail size and native size.

| Criterion | Weight | Blind 1 | Blind 2 |

| Face-reference fidelity | 25 | 24 | 23 |
| Nekomimi / maid / tsundere legibility | 25 | 21 | 25 |
| Photorealism and anatomy | 20 | 19 | 18 |
| Composition and lighting coherence | 15 | 14 | 14 |
| Defect and forbidden-element control | 10 | 10 | 9 |
| Pixel subtotal | 95 | 88 | 89 |

## Blind 1 observations

- Strongest face and hair resemblance to the supplied reference.
- Cat ears and maid uniform read immediately; clean, polished café portrait.
- Crossed arms and sideways glance imply aloofness, but the tsundere contradiction is comparatively generic and lacks a visible directed act.
- Hands, garment edges, background, and text-free constraint are clean.

## Blind 2 observations

- Strong face and hair resemblance, though the pout and gaze change the reference expression more than Blind 1.
- Best thumbnail-level concept communication: cat ears, maid uniform, averted pout, and offering a warm cup make the outward-cold/inward-caring contradiction readable without metadata.
- The recipient creates a clear action/target/consequence relation and a more dynamic frame.
- Slight softness/ambiguity around the cup-holding hand and foreground transfer costs some anatomy/defect points.

The remaining 5 points for prompt discipline and process evidence are intentionally deferred until after condition reveal.
